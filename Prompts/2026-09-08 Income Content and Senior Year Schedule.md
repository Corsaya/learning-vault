# Income, Content, and Senior Year Schedule

## Prompt

Interview Donovan to find a school-year effort that can compound into income,
after card flipping failed to produce repeatable profit. Evaluate content
creation (mass uploading versus quality), his goals, current schedule, skills,
equipment, work, crew, college applications, Japan target, and Melee goals. Build
a realistic schedule, save the resulting documents in the vaults, prepare the
schedule for Google Calendar, commit, and push.

User-supplied constraints included school 7:20 AM–2 PM; fall/winter crew Tuesday
through Thursday 2:30–5:30 PM and Saturday 8–11:30 AM; restaurant work Friday,
Saturday, and Sunday; 10:45 PM–6:45 AM sleep target; AP Calculus, Honors World
Literature, Theology IV, and AP Physics; October FAFSA/SAT dates; pending college
applications; a top-three SRAA target; beginner Yoshi improvement; a $3,000–$5,000
Japan concept; and an existing recording/editing setup.

## Result

- Created a fall operating plan through November 15 with fixed commitments,
  priority blocks, cut rules, weekly scoreboard, and application deadlines.
- Flagged Clemson Spring 2027 and Tennessee Summer 2027 entry terms for immediate
  verification because Donovan graduates in June 2027.
- Verified that Notre Dame REA permits other nonbinding EA applications.
- Created a 90-day quality-first content experiment, equipment audit, skill
  benchmark, income ranking, job-replacement test, and kill rules.
- Created a private Japan/college savings model without treating content revenue
  as guaranteed.
- Created an `.ics` import file. Direct upload was not performed because no
  Google Calendar connector or configured local CalDAV/calendar client was
  available in the session.
