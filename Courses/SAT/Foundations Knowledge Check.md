# SAT Foundations Knowledge Check — 2026-08-08

Not a timed test, not multiple choice. This is a full inventory of every rule,
formula, and question-type across both SAT sections, checked one at a time:
**do you know the actual steps to solve it, or would you be guessing on test
day?** Gaps found here become short lessons (same format as
[[Diagnostic Gap Lessons (2026-08-07)]]). Once this is clean, the plan is a
full timed official Bluebook practice test.

Organized by College Board's official domains (see
[[Research/Official SAT Structure and Content Research (2026-08-07)]] for
the weighting numbers). Status column fills in as we go through each item
live in conversation — not self-graded in isolation.

**Legend:** ✅ know it cold · ⚠️ shaky/slow · ❌ don't know · ⬜ not yet checked
