## 1. Official structure

2h 14min total test time + ~10min untimed break.

| Section | Modules | Q/module | Time/module | Total Qs | Total time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reading & Writing | 2 | 27 | 32 min | 54 | 64 min |
| Math | 2 | 22 | 35 min | 44 | 70 min |

Each module has 2 unscored pretest questions mixed in, unidentifiable to the student.

**Adaptive mechanic:** Module 1 (broad difficulty mix) routes to a harder or easier Module 2
based on performance. Harder Module 2 = full 800 scoring ceiling unlocked; easier Module 2 =
capped max score. Exact cap numbers are **not published by College Board** — circulating
prep-community figures (~560–630 range depending on source) disagree with each other; don't
treat any specific number as authoritative.

Source: [How the SAT Is Structured – College Board](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/whats-on-the-test/structure)

## 3. Officially released practice materials

- **Bluebook full-length practice tests**: College Board doesn't publish an exact current
  count on their own site. Prep-community consensus (consistent across sources, not
  College-Board-confirmed): **Tests 4–11 currently available** (Tests 1–3 retired), Test 7
  the last "fully new" 2025 test, Test 11 newest (~Feb 2026). **Verify the live count directly
  in Bluebook before relying on this number for course planning.**
- **Student Question Bank (SQB)**: official, via Bluebook/satsuite — thousands of real
  retired questions, filterable by section/domain/skill/difficulty. Best free official source
  for domain-targeted drilling (i.e., Advanced Math + Conventions practice sets).
  https://bluebook.collegeboard.org/students/practice
- **SAT Suite Educator Question Bank**: https://satsuiteeducatorquestionbank.collegeboard.org/
  — 3,500+ real released questions, exportable, educator-tier access. Cleanest ToS-safe
  bulk-export source if access is available.
- **No legitimate "leaked" digital SAT content exists** — the digital SAT's adaptive item-pool
  architecture (multistage adaptive testing) means there's no single fixed "form" to leak the
  way there was on paper. Anything claiming to be "leaked digital SAT questions" on
  Telegram/Discord/etc. is either repackaged Bluebook content or mislabeled old paper-SAT
  material — don't build course content from these.
- **OnePrep** (oneprep.com) — free, 4,000+ tutor-authored questions styled to match Bluebook's
  UI/difficulty. Legitimate supplementary volume, but **not official College Board content** —
  label it as such if used in the course.

## 5. Test-taking strategy consensus (cross-checked, not single-source)

- **Skip-and-flag** is officially supported (Bluebook's "Mark for Review" tool) and
  universally recommended across independent prep sources: set a rough per-question time
  ceiling (~45–75s R&W, ~90–100s Math), flag and move on, return with remaining time. No
  penalty for guessing, all questions equal-weighted — protects access to easier later
  questions.
- **Desmos**: officially available for the *entire* Math section (unlike the old paper SAT's
  calculator-restricted portion). Strategy consensus across sources: *selective*, not maximal,
  use is the actual score-maximizing pattern — over-reliance costs time on ~10–15
  questions/test better solved by hand. Rule of thumb: if the setup types in cleanly in one
  line, use Desmos; if the setup itself is the hard part, don't.
- **Elimination-based reading**: officially supported via the built-in "Option Eliminator"
  tool, but *how* to best use it strategically was thin across sources checked — flagged as
  an area needing a deeper follow-up pass before building specific course guidance around it.
- **Pacing benchmark** (prep-community consensus, not official): ~71 sec/question R&W,
  ~95 sec/question Math, aiming to bank a few minutes for flagged-question review.

## What's official vs. prep-community (course-building caution)

**Solid (College Board's own docs):** structure/timing, domain weightings, registration/test
dates, Mark-for-Review + Option-Eliminator tool existence, Desmos section-wide availability,
equating rationale against curve rumors.

**Prep-community-sourced (flag as such in course material):** exact adaptive score-cap
numbers, current Bluebook test count/history, specific pacing-second benchmarks, Desmos
selective-use tactical rule, skip-and-flag time thresholds, any curve claims.

**Unresolved:** question-pool overlap between August 2026 and recently retired forms.
