# Bluebook Tools and Pacing Reference

What the Bluebook app actually gives you during the test, plus the pacing and guessing consensus.

> Trimmed 2026-08-20 — Khan Academy, third-party books, social-media advice, and the
> homemade-mock build spec were removed. **Bluebook and the College Board Question Bank are
> the only sources now.**

## 1. Scoring

Total 400–1600, section 200–800 each. Raw score = **number correct** — there is **no
wrong-answer penalty**. Raw is then scaled by IRT equating that accounts for which Module 2
difficulty tier you were routed into.

**Consequence: never leave anything blank.** A blank is a guaranteed zero; a guess is a
free 25% shot.

## 2. The tools you get in Bluebook

- **Desmos calculator** — the real Desmos engine, licensed and embedded, available for the
  **entire** Math section (both modules), not a College Board clone.
- **Math reference sheet — geometry only** (~15 formulas: area/volume, Pythagorean theorem,
  special right triangles, circle constants). Algebra, functions, statistics, and most of
  geometry are **not** on it and must be memorized. See the 📄/🧠 split in
  [[../Quick Reference/Quick Reference — Math Formulas and Grammar Rules|Quick Reference]].
- **Mark for Review** — a per-question flag, with a color-coded status bar always visible and
  a full review screen at the end of each module (flagged / unanswered / answered) plus
  jump-to-flagged before you submit.
- **Option Eliminator ("cross-out")** — toggled on by an icon, strikes out a choice per
  question, fully undoable. A real interactive tool, not just a mental habit — use it.
- **Annotation / highlighting** — highlights or underlines passage and question text.
- **Line Reader** — a draggable "digital ruler" that isolates one line at a time.

## 3. Pacing

Community consensus (corroborated across independent sources; not College Board-published):

| Section | Per question |
|---|---|
| R&W — reading passages | ~85–90 sec |
| R&W — standalone grammar | ~45–60 sec |
| Math | ~90–100 sec |

## 4. Guessing and flagging order

Finish **every** question in the module first — **guess-and-flag, never blank-and-flag.**
Then work back through the flagged items **most-confident first, least-confident last**, so
you bank the recoverable points before the time sinks. Matches the three-pass approach in
[[../Test Strategy/Test Day Execution|Test Day Execution]].
