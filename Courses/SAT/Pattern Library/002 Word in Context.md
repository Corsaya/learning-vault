# Pattern 002 — Word in Context → Predict Before You Look

Difficulty

Medium

Estimated Solve Time

30-45 seconds

Domain

Craft and Structure

Subskill

Words in Context

Evidence

Four misses on [[Bluebook Practice Test — 2026-08-20]] — M1 Q3, M2 Q2, M2 Q3, M2 Q4.
The largest single cluster on that test.

---

## Recognition

Use this pattern whenever the question reads

"most logical and precise word or phrase"

or

"most nearly means"

---

## Core Idea

The blank is decided by the **logic of the sentence**, not the topic of the passage.

Every wrong answer is a real word that fits the subject matter.

---

## Algorithm

Step 1

Cover the four choices. Do not read them yet.

Step 2

Find the logical signal.

although / but / yet / however / paradox / despite

These force a **reversal**. The blank must be negative if the setup is positive.

Step 3

Say your own word out loud.

Step 4

Uncover the choices. Match to your word.

Step 5

If two choices both fit your word, your word was too vague. Re-read the clause.

---

## Memory Rule

Cover

↓

Predict

↓

Match

---

## Why It Works

The distractors are built from the passage's topic.

They are chosen to look right to a reader who skipped step 3.

Predicting first makes the topical bait visible.

---

## SAT Traps

Picking the everyday sense of a word that has a second, older sense.

"manifest" = perceptible, not realized.

Picking a neutral word when the sentence demands a negative one.

"reflected" when the paradox needs "hampered."

Reading the choices first.
