# Pattern 004 — "Uses Data from the Table" → Find the Claim First

Difficulty

Medium-Hard

Estimated Solve Time

60-75 seconds

Domain

Information and Ideas

Subskill

Quantitative Evidence

Evidence

Two misses on [[Bluebook Practice Test — 2026-08-20]] — M1 Q13 and M2 Q12. Both
attempted, both wrong.

---

## Recognition

Use this pattern whenever the question says

"most effectively uses data from the table"

or

"most effectively completes the example"

---

## Core Idea

The table is not the question.

The sentence before the blank states a **claim**.

The right answer is the data that supports **that** claim.

---

## Algorithm

Step 1

Ignore the table completely.

Step 2

Read the sentence containing the blank and the one before it.

Step 3

Write down what has to be true.

"Navajo-Churro inner fiber is softer than mouflon's or Merino's."

Step 4

Now go to the table with that specific comparison in mind.

Step 5

Match. Verify the numbers actually say it.

---

## Memory Rule

Claim first

Table second

---

## Why It Works

Every wrong choice quotes **real numbers from the table**.

They are accurate and irrelevant.

Accuracy is not the test. Relevance is.

---

## SAT Traps

A choice that reads correct numbers but supports the opposite claim.

A choice that compares the wrong two rows.

A choice that restates the table without completing the argument.

Going to the table first — this is the error that produced both misses.
