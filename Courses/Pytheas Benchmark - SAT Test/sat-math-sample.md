### SAT Math — Linear Equations and Systems (Sample Source)

#### Key concept: Solving systems of linear equations

A system of two linear equations can be solved by substitution or elimination. Example:
2x + y = 11 x - y = 1
Adding the two equations eliminates y: 3x = 12, so x = 4. Substituting back: 4 - y = 1, so y = 3. Solution: (4, 3).

#### Key concept: Slope-intercept form

y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept. Parallel lines have equal slopes; perpendicular lines have slopes that are negative reciprocals of each other.

#### Key concept: Word problems as linear models

If a plumber charges a $75 flat fee plus $60 per hour, total cost C after h hours is C = 60h + 75. The SAT frequently asks you to identify which part of a word problem is the slope vs. the intercept.

#### Practice problem

A rental car company charges a $40 base fee plus $0.25 per mile driven. Write an equation for total cost C in terms of miles driven m, and find the cost of driving 120 miles.
