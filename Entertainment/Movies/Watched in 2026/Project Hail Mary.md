Watched: 4-6-26
@ Ventnor Square Theater
Rating: 8.9/10
Not a bad scene in this film. The introduction was intriguing, and the formula for the flashbacks happening as he regains his memory are very cool. All flashback scenes had me engaged. The visuals were spectacular, as well as the score. Ryan Gosling had an amazing performance, especially for being in almost 75% of the movie. A breath of fresh air from Iron Lung, but still cramped when the event calls for it. There were times that I was kind of close to tears, just from the magnificence in visuals, storytelling, or suspense. One of the best movies of the decade.
