## Team-by-team audit

### 1. LeGOAT

Current roster: **A+**

Draft sensibility: **A+**

Opening picks: Jahmyr Gibbs, Christian McCaffrey, Amon-Ra St. Brown, Nico Collins, Drake London.

This was the most sensible draft and produced the strongest roster. LeGOAT accumulated premium RB/WR value first, waited until Round 8 for Jalen Hurts, and waited until Rounds 12 and 14 for defense and kicker. Breece Hall in Round 9 and Bucky Irving in Round 10 were extreme values.

There is no meaningful structural error to criticize.

### 2. Team LiamDo

Current roster: **A-**

Draft sensibility: **A**

Opening picks: Jaxon Smith-Njigba, De'Von Achane, Trey McBride, Chase Brown, Kyren Williams.

This was another coherent draft. LiamDo secured a WR anchor, three starting-caliber RBs, and an elite TE before taking Drake Maye in Round 8. Kicker and defense waited until Rounds 11 and 12.

The primary weakness is merely that LeGOAT extracted even more value.

### 3. Robert Rows

Current roster: **B+**

Draft sensibility: **A-**

Opening picks: Jonathan Taylor, Justin Jefferson, CeeDee Lamb, Malik Nabers, Javonte Williams.

Four premium RB/WR picks before addressing QB and TE was structurally sound. The Jefferson–Lamb–Nabers combination is the league's best receiver room.

Taking a second QB in Round 9 and second TE in Round 11 was somewhat redundant, but neither decision seriously damaged the roster.

### 4. Team babylelilmandihh

Current roster: **B-**

Draft sensibility: **B-**

Opening picks: Aaron Rodgers, Saquon Barkley, James Cook, Omarion Hampton, Rashee Rice.

Rodgers at 1.04 was indefensible in this format. However, the remaining draft was mostly rational: three strong RBs, several viable receivers, Tyler Warren, George Kittle, and late defense/kicker selections.

This was a good draft attached to one terrible first-round decision.

### 5. Team robsteribbleparlays

Current roster: **C**

Draft sensibility: **C+**

Opening picks: Lil'Jordan Humphrey, Puka Nacua, Christian Kirk, Brock Bowers, Cam Skattebo.

Lil'Jordan Humphrey at 1.08 effectively forfeited the first-round selection. After that, Puka, Bowers, DeVonta Smith, Baker Mayfield, and several RBs created a coherent roster.

The process after Round 1 was respectable. The current problem is extremely thin WR depth.

### 6. Team Alexander0261

Current roster: **B**

Draft sensibility: **C**

Opening picks: Ja'Marr Chase, Tom Brady, Ashton Jeanty, Derrick Henry, Joe Burrow.

Brady at 2.03 was a dead pick. Philadelphia DEF in Round 6, Brandon Aubrey in Round 7, and three drafted quarterbacks consumed additional capital unnecessarily.

Chase, Jeanty, Henry, Burrow, McLaurin, Davante Adams, and Mark Andrews rescued the roster. The current team is better than the draft process deserved.

### 7. Team LiveLoveLe

Current roster: **B+**

Draft sensibility: **C-**

Opening picks: Bijan Robinson, Rob Gronkowski, A.J. Brown, Lamar Jackson, Kenneth Walker.

Gronkowski at 2.06 was a dead selection. Drafting three quarterbacks behind Lamar, taking Baltimore DEF in Round 6, and using Round 8 on a kicker compounded the inefficiency.

The roster remains good because Bijan, A.J. Brown, Lamar, Kenneth Walker, Garrett Wilson, and Travis Etienne hit. Keeping Gronkowski and three QBs on the current roster is still wasting bench capacity.

### 8. Team Gassy

Current roster: **C-**

Draft sensibility: **D**

Opening picks: Josh Allen, Travis Hunter, Cameron Dicker, Rams DEF.

Allen at 1.01 was a major opportunity-cost loss when Lamar lasted until Round 4, Burrow until Round 5, and Hurts and Maye until Round 8. Hunter at 2.08 currently projects for approximately three offensive points. A kicker in Round 3 and defense in Round 4 surrendered two more premium RB/WR picks.

The later draft was considerably better:

- Ladd McConkey in Round 5
- David Montgomery in Round 6
- TreVeyon Henderson in Round 8
- Jameson Williams in Round 12
- Sam LaPorta in Round 13

The draft was not fourteen bad selections. It was four damaging opening selections followed by a decent recovery. DK Metcalf for Kenyon Sadiq was a sensible first repair, but RB strength and bench utilization remain the central problems.

## Final judgment

LeGOAT, LiamDo, and Robert Rows used recognizable positional-value strategies. Babylelilmandihh and robsteribbleparlays each survived one disastrous opening pick. Alexander0261 and LiveLoveLe produced good current rosters despite inefficient construction.

Team Gassy had the league's least sensible draft because the mistakes were consecutive and consumed all four premium selections. Its current roster is better than a typical D-grade draft would produce because the late-round WR/TE choices landed and DK was available afterward.

The season remains recoverable, but recovery requires active waivers and at least one RB substantially outperforming current expectations.
