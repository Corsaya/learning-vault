# Lil' Oats — Weekly Analysis Screenshot Checklist

Send the screenshots below. If one item takes several screenshots, keep them together and include the item number in your message. Screenshots should show the full page, player names, teams, positions, status labels, projections, and scoring totals wherever available.

## One-time preseason setup

1. League overview: platform, league name, number of teams, league type (redraft, keeper, or dynasty), draft format, and draft date.
2. Complete scoring settings: passing, rushing, receiving, kicking, defense/special teams, bonuses, penalties, points per reception, points per first down, and fractional scoring.
3. Roster and lineup settings: every starting slot, bench size, IR slots, taxi squad, maximum players by position, and whether lineups lock by player or at the first game.
4. Waiver and free-agent rules: FAAB or priority, budget, tie-breaker, processing days/times, continuous or game-time waivers, free-agent availability, and whether dropped players are locked.
5. Trade rules: deadline, review or veto process, review period, draft-pick trading, future-year limits, trade fees, and any roster restrictions.
6. Playoff and schedule rules: playoff teams, playoff weeks, byes, seeding, reseeding, tiebreakers, consolation bracket, two-week matchups, divisions, and median-game or extra-matchup rules.
7. Keeper or dynasty rules, if applicable: keeper count and cost, contracts, salaries, rookie draft, taxi rules, draft-pick ownership, deadlines, and offseason roster limits.
8. Full draft results: the entire draft board in pick order, including traded picks, keepers, auction prices, and each manager's team name.
9. Lil' Oats roster immediately after the draft, including starters, bench, IR/taxi, acquisition method, and auction salary or keeper cost if relevant.
10. Every opponent's full roster immediately after the draft, including starters, bench, IR/taxi, and salaries or keeper costs if relevant.
11. Initial league schedule and matchup calendar for the full regular season and playoffs.
12. Initial standings, waiver priority or FAAB balances, and any power rankings or projections supplied by the platform.
13. League constitution and any commissioner rules, house rules, punishments, side pots, rivalry rules, or settings not visible elsewhere.

## Send before each week's waiver run

14. Current Lil' Oats roster: starters, bench, IR/taxi, player status, projected points, and season totals.
15. Previous week's Lil' Oats matchup: both teams' complete starters and benches, projected and actual points, and final score.
16. Current standings: records, points for, points against, streaks, divisions, median results, and playoff probabilities if shown.
17. Current waiver priority and/or every team's remaining FAAB balance.
18. Available quarterbacks, including projected points, roster percentage, upcoming opponent, and status.
19. Available running backs with the same details.
20. Available wide receivers with the same details.
21. Available tight ends with the same details.
22. Available kickers and defenses/special teams with the same details.
23. Recent league transactions since the last analysis: adds, drops, waiver bids, trades, IR moves, and failed claims if the platform reveals them.
24. Current trade block and any offers received or sent, including all players, picks, FAAB, deadlines, and messages or context.
25. Every opponent's current roster, especially injured players, positional needs, excess depth, players on the trade block, and recent additions or drops.
26. Lil' Oats' upcoming matchup: both projected lineups, benches, projections, player status, and win probability.
27. Rest-of-season schedule or matchup-strength view for Lil' Oats players, if the platform provides one.
28. Any important platform news screens: injuries, practice participation, depth-chart changes, suspensions, role changes, and estimated return dates.
29. Any lineup, waiver, or trade decision you are already considering, written in the message alongside the screenshots.
30. Your priorities and constraints for the week: floor versus upside, players you will not trade, acceptable FAAB spending, record-related urgency, and risk tolerance.

## Send after waivers and before the first game

31. Waiver results for the entire league, including winning bids, losing bids if visible, priority changes, and all corresponding drops.
32. Updated Lil' Oats roster after waivers and free-agent moves.
33. Updated available-player lists if meaningful players remain or new players were dropped.
34. Updated injury designations, practice reports, game statuses, and expected availability for every relevant rostered player.
35. Final matchup projection and both teams' expected starting lineups.
36. Any new trade offers, accepted trades, commissioner actions, scoring corrections, or rule changes.

## Send during or after the week when something changes

37. Thursday or early-game results that affect remaining start/sit decisions.
38. Late injury news, inactive lists, weather concerns, depth-chart changes, or surprise roster moves.
39. Any last-minute lineup changes made by Lil' Oats or the opponent.
40. Final matchup result once every game and scoring correction is complete, including starters, bench, optimal-lineup information if available, and final score.

## Analysis deliverables each week

The weekly analysis will cover lineup decisions, bench and drop candidates, waiver targets and bid ranges, trade targets and offers, rest-of-season roster construction, opponent and matchup strategy, injury contingencies, playoff positioning, and a review of the previous week's decisions.
