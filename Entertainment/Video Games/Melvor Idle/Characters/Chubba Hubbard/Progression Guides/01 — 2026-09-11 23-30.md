## Ordered route

Do these in order. Farming checks happen in parallel and do not replace the
numbered active task.

### 1. Stabilize the bank and farm

- [ ] Leave at least **three free bank slots** before beginning Mining; it adds
  Iron Ore and Coal Ore, and Smithing adds Iron and Steel Bars.
- [ ] Harvest the current Potatoes when ready, compost, and immediately replant
  an allotment. Use the lowest-tier crop that can be replanted sustainably.
- [ ] Plant the Oak Tree Seed when the first tree plot is available; tree crops
  give Farming XP without consuming the active-skill slot.
- [ ] Keep the one Strawberry Seed. The wiki specifically flags Strawberry
  Seeds as an item not to sell casually.
- [ ] Do not sell Emeralds or Diamonds merely to clear a slot. They are useful
  in later crafting, potion, summoning, and upgrade routes.

If fewer than three slots remain, sell easy-to-replace inputs in this order:
Tin Ore once the Bronze block is finished, Potatoes beyond the next planting
and food need, then excess Normal Logs. Do not buy a bank **tab** for space—a
tab only reorganizes existing slots.

### 2. Train Mining 6 → 30

- [ ] Mine Copper/Tin only until enough Bronze Bars exist to move Smithing off
  level 1. Do not build a giant Bronze stockpile.
- [ ] At **Mining 10**, buy the **Steel Pickaxe for 2,000 GP**.
- [ ] At **Mining 15**, switch to Iron Ore and bank a working supply.
- [ ] At **Mining 20**, buy the **Black Pickaxe for 10,000 GP**.
- [ ] Continue to **Mining 30**, then mine Coal when Steel production actually
  needs it. Steel Bars use one Iron Ore and two Coal Ore.
- [ ] Keep gems from ordinary Mining. Do not buy Gem Gloves yet; the 500,000 GP
  entry cost is ten times the current cash pile.

Why 30: it unlocks Coal and creates a clean handoff into Steel later. Why stop
there: pushing Mining much higher before Smithing or combat only recreates the
Fishing imbalance in a different color.

### 3. Train Smithing 1 → 27

- [ ] Smith the existing Bronze Bars, then make only enough Bronze Bars/items
  to reach **Smithing 10**.
- [ ] At 10, smelt Iron Bars. Prefer the highest useful Iron item available as
  new recipes unlock; avoid making thousands of low-value Bronze items.
- [ ] Make an Iron weapon and armour pieces as their recipes unlock: Dagger 10,
  Scimitar 15, Helmet 16, Boots 19, Shield 21, Platelegs 25, Platebody 27.
- [ ] Stop at **Smithing 27** with an Iron weapon, Helmet, Boots, Shield,
  Platelegs, and Platebody banked or equipped.
- [ ] Preserve remaining Iron Ore for the later Steel block. Steel Bar smelting
  unlocks at Smithing 25, but a full Steel equipment set is not needed before
  learning basic combat.

Do not buy Smithing Gloves for this short block. At 100,000 GP, their +60%
Smithing XP charges are better saved for a long, supplied Smithing session.

### 4. Learn melee manually

- [ ] Equip the best completed Iron gear, the existing Pirate's Lost Ring, and
  one cooked-food stack.
- [ ] Fight Plants first. Use **Stab** until Attack 10, **Slash** until Strength
  10, then **Block** until Defence 10.
- [ ] Manually watch HP and eat. **Auto Eat is not owned**, so this is not an
  unattended-combat block.
- [ ] Take an Easy Slayer task only when its target is currently safe. Do not
  buy Auto Slayer before the account can survive whatever task it rolls.
- [ ] Loot between fights before the loot container fills. The Amulet of
  Looting comes later from Spider Forest.

Attack affects accuracy, Strength affects maximum hit, and Defence affects
melee evasion. Raising the three together teaches the combat screen while
avoiding a one-stat account.

## Skill-by-skill plan

These sections prevent a forgotten skill from disappearing from the route. “Hold”
means the efficient decision for this save is to leave the skill alone for now.

### Woodcutting — 52

**Hold.** The account already has 15,456 Normal Logs and discovered the Ent
mark. When Woodcutting resumes, buy the Black and Mithril Axes first, equip Ent
tablets if available, then cut the highest useful log—not merely the highest XP
tree. Mahogany unlocks at 55 and feeds later Herblore, Summoning, and upgrades.

### Firemaking — 1

**Later support block.** Raise it to 10 before the next serious Cooking session,
then buy the Oak Cooking Fire. Do not burn logs primarily for Coal; the wiki
notes direct Mining is more efficient. Firemaking 99 is valuable eventually for
the Skillcape's +5% global skill XP, not before the current melee foundation.

### Fishing — 58

**Hold.** The account owns through the Adamant Fishing Rod and has 15,456 Raw
Trout. Return when a specific fish, special drop, or GP method is needed. At 60,
the Rune Fishing Rod costs 300,000 GP; buy it only when Fishing resumes.

### Cooking — 42

**Hold.** There are 1,297 cooked Trout plus 1,438 equipped Shrimp/Perfect
Shrimp. That is enough for the manual early-combat block. Before another large
batch, raise Firemaking and purchase the best affordable Cooking Fire; Cooking
Fires grant cumulative Cooking XP bonuses.

### Mining — 6

**Primary skill now.** Follow the 6 → 30 route above. Buy Steel and Black
Pickaxes during the block. Ordinary ore Mining also has a chance to produce
gems, so it advances both equipment and longer-term supplies.

### Smithing — 1

**Primary skill after Mining.** Reach 27 and produce the complete Iron setup.
Mining → bars → equipment is the account's missing production chain.

### Thieving — 1

**Hold.** It can later supply GP, seeds, and useful drops, but failed picks and
low stealth make level 1 inefficient. Revisit after basic food/combat and buy
Thieving Gloves only for a sustained session where the added stealth changes
the success rate materially.

### Farming — 1

**Always active in parallel.** Harvest and replant whenever checked. Allotments
use three seeds, herbs two, and trees one. Compost to 100% survival until crop
mastery makes that unnecessary. Farming consumes no active-skill time, so an
empty usable plot is wasted compounding.

### Fletching — 1

**Hold until Ranged preparation.** Woodcutting supplies logs; Smithing supplies
arrowtips. Start only when there is a specific ammunition/weapon target so it
does not create extra bank stacks without a use.

### Crafting — 1

**Hold until Ranged or jewelry requires it.** Preserve useful gems and hides.
Crafting eventually supplies stronger Ranged armour and bonus-providing items,
but it is not required for the first melee checkpoint.

### Runecrafting — 4

**Hold.** The bank has only 11 Rune Essence and 19 Air Runes. When Magic becomes
the target, mine a deliberate essence stockpile, craft elemental runes, then
make a staff to reduce repeated elemental rune costs.

### Herblore — 1

**Hold while Farming starts herbs.** Plant herbs as soon as a herb plot and seed
supply allow it. Start Herblore when enough ingredients exist to make a useful
batch rather than producing a few charges and another bank stack.

### Agility — 1

**Hold.** Obstacles cost GP and materials and often include trade-offs. Build
only when the chosen obstacle benefits the next long training block; do not
spend scarce early resources for a generic completion checkbox.

### Summoning — 1

**Small optional setup.** The Ent mark is discovered. Craft at least one batch
of Ent tablets when bank room allows, because additional Ent marks cannot drop
until the familiar's tablets have entered the bank. Equip them during the next
Woodcutting block so Summoning XP and Woodcutting benefits occur together.

### Astrology — 6

**Hold for now; never forget it.** Save normal and Golden Stardust. Return for a
deliberate constellation block once the desired modifiers are known; do not
spend Golden Stardust randomly for small early bonuses.

### Township — 26

**Passive check-in only.** Claim useful tasks and prevent capped resources when
convenient, but do not redirect the active route or spend heavily without
checking the current building return. Township mechanics have changed across
versions, so use the v1.3.1 wiki before a large rebuild.

## Combat plan

### Attack — 2

Train with **Stab** to 10. Accuracy is more useful than raw max hit if attacks
are missing constantly. Switch styles intentionally; weapon ownership alone
does not decide which melee stat gains XP.

### Strength — 1

Train with **Slash** to 10 after Attack. Strength raises maximum melee hit and
therefore kill speed once accuracy is adequate.

### Defence — 1

Train with **Block** to 10 after Attack and Strength. Defence raises melee
evasion and unlocks later armour tiers; Iron armour is already wearable at
level 1 and Steel at level 5 in v1.3.1.

### Hitpoints — 10

It rises naturally from dealing combat damage. Do not grind it separately at
this stage. More HP helps, but idle safety depends on the exact enemy maximum
hit, damage reduction, food, and Auto Eat threshold—not HP level alone.

### Ranged — 1

Hold until Fletching/Smithing can support a real ammunition stack. Ranged is
mandatory later because the combat triangle matters in the God Dungeons.

### Magic — 1

Hold until Runecrafting supplies a staff and sufficient runes. Magic is strong
against melee enemies and Alt. Magic can provide utility, but a tiny rune stack
would make this a stop-start session now.

### Prayer — 1

Preserve bones once combat starts. Prayer points are consumed resources; use
prayers when their accuracy, damage, or protection benefit changes the fight,
not on enemies already dying safely.

### Slayer — 1

Accept safe Easy tasks while learning combat so kills also earn Slayer XP and
Slayer Coins. Do not automate tasks yet. Slayer XP is earned from Slayer tasks
or Slayer-area monsters, not from arbitrary combat alone.

### Next combat milestones after this guide

1. Build melee stats and equipment toward Steel/Mithril.
2. Reach 1,000,000 GP and buy Auto Eat I before unattended combat.
3. Clear beginner dungeons manually.
4. Farm Spider Forest for the Amulet of Looting only when the exact setup is
   safe; check an up-to-date calculator before idling.
5. Develop Ranged and Magic rather than forcing melee through the combat
   triangle.

## Keep / sell rules

### Keep

- Strawberry Seeds and scarce tree/herb seeds
- Emeralds and Diamonds; keep at least a useful reserve of other gems
- Stardust and Golden Stardust
- Ancient Ring of Skills/Mastery, Gold Topaz Ring, signet halves, Circlet/Jewel
  of Rhaelyx, Amulet of Fishing, mastery tokens, unique drops, and keys
- Enough ore/bars for the next named equipment or shop target

### Sell when bank pressure requires it

- Obsolete low-tier ore after its planned Smithing block
- Easily regathered food beyond the next combat reserve
- Excess Normal Logs beyond the next Firemaking/Fletching/Summoning target
- Common junk with no named task, upgrade, or near-term recipe

Sell by purpose, not by stack size. A one-item rare stack may be more important
than 10,000 replaceable logs.
