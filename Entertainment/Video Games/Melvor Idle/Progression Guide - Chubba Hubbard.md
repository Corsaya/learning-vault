# Chubba Hubbard — progression guide

> [!summary] Do this next
> **Fish → sell selected catches → expand the bank → start Farming → mine/smith melee gear → train on Slayer tasks.**
>
> For the next offline session, fish the best fish available in **Shrapnel River**. Keep enough raw fish to raise Cooking alongside Fishing; sell the surplus for GP. Do not grind more shrimp just because its mastery is high.

This is a milestone route, not a rigid speedrun. It was tailored to the exported save from **2026-09-10 12:00 PM** and the base-game **v1.3.1** rules.

The account has already solved its immediate food problem. Its bottlenecks are now **GP, bank space, gear, and passive-skill momentum**.

## Phase 1 — stabilize the account

- [ ] Open the **Treasure Chest** before the next long session; keep an Amulet of Fishing if it appears.
- [ ] Claim the Fishing and Cooking mastery tokens unless saving them for deliberate mastery-pool management.
- [ ] Fish the highest-XP useful fish currently available, moving up whenever a better catch unlocks.
- [ ] Cook a working reserve of roughly **2,000–5,000 food**, preferably better fish than shrimp as they unlock.
- [ ] Sell only surplus fish and ordinary junk for early GP. Keep gems, special fishing drops, Stardust, Golden Stardust, and unfamiliar one-off items.
- [ ] Buy affordable bank slots before the bank becomes full; a full bank can cause newly obtained items to be lost.
- [ ] Keep Farming running whenever seeds are available. Use compost early, when failed crops would consume scarce seeds.

### Why this is first

Fishing is already the account's strongest gathering skill, Cooking is keeping pace, and the Fishing mastery is concentrated heavily in Shrimp. Continuing to fish is the shortest route to both food and starter cash, but staying on Shrimp would over-invest in a low-value catch. Move upward through unlocked fish.

## Phase 2 — build the melee foundation

- [ ] Raise **Mining to 15**, prioritizing Copper/Tin only as needed and then Iron.
- [ ] Raise **Smithing to 15** and make a full Iron set plus an Iron Scimitar or Sword.
- [ ] Continue Mining/Smithing toward **Steel (30 Defence/Attack gear requirements)** as combat catches up.
- [ ] Mine coal directly when needed; do not use Firemaking as the main coal source.
- [ ] Train Attack to the next weapon requirement, then Strength for damage, and Defence for the armour tier.
- [ ] Prefer **Slayer tasks** over random monsters once basic gear and food are equipped, so combat time also earns Slayer XP and Slayer Coins.

Suggested early melee balance:

1. Attack 10 for Iron weapons.
2. Strength 10–20 for faster kills.
3. Defence 10 for Iron armour.
4. Attack/Strength/Defence toward 30 for Steel equipment, using the combat screen's hit chance and incoming damage to decide which stat lags.

> [!warning] Idle-combat rule
> Do not leave combat unattended merely because plenty of food is equipped. Until **Auto Eat** is purchased, food does not make unattended combat safe. After buying Auto Eat, check the enemy's maximum hit against the Auto Eat threshold before idling.

## Phase 3 — unlock compounding systems

Run these alongside the melee path rather than postponing them indefinitely.

### Farming

- [ ] Get seeds from Farmers in the Farmlands or Bird Nests from Woodcutting.
- [ ] Plant every time the game is checked; Farming runs without occupying the active-skill slot.
- [ ] Preserve seeds until each crop becomes self-sustaining.
- [ ] Begin herbs early so Herblore is not blocked later.

### Astrology

- [ ] Raise Astrology to unlock the constellation supporting the skill currently being trained.
- [ ] Spend normal Stardust on useful, broadly applicable bonuses.
- [ ] Conserve Golden Stardust until the desired high-impact modifiers are clear.

### Summoning and Herblore

- [ ] Train each enough to discover marks and unlock basic tablets/potions.
- [ ] Create tablets after discovering marks; marks beyond the first require the familiar's tablet recipe to be unlocked.
- [ ] Use potions and familiars while training their related skills instead of saving every charge forever.

### Township

- [ ] Read the current Township mechanics before investing heavily; it has changed substantially across versions.
- [ ] Treat it as a passive support system, not the immediate solution to the account's GP shortage.

## Phase 4 — first major combat milestones

Progress one checkpoint at a time. Re-check equipment, food, damage reduction, and maximum enemy hit before every unattended dungeon grind.

- [ ] Buy **Auto Eat I**, then later II and III as affordable.
- [ ] Clear beginner dungeons manually.
- [ ] Farm **Spider Forest** for the Amulet of Looting.
- [ ] Build upgraded Mithril/Adamant/Dragon melee gear rather than heavily investing in trimmed low-tier armour.
- [ ] Develop Ranged and Magic; the combat triangle becomes mandatory later.
- [ ] Clear **Volcanic Cave** once the idle calculation is safe.
- [ ] Farm Ancient gear and prepare all three styles for the God Dungeons.
- [ ] Follow dungeon order: Air → Water → Earth → Fire, checking the individual guide for each.
- [ ] Continue through Into the Mist and Impending Darkness only after the base-game combat foundations are complete.

## Long-term skill order

There is no single required order, but this minimizes dead ends for this save:

1. **Fishing/Cooking** — immediate GP and food.
2. **Mining/Smithing** — melee equipment and bars.
3. **Farming** — always-on food, herbs, and seeds.
4. **Combat/Slayer** — drops, dungeon unlocks, Slayer equipment.
5. **Woodcutting/Fletching** — ranged weapons and ammunition.
6. **Runecrafting/Magic** — runes, magic gear, Alt. Magic utility.
7. **Herblore/Summoning/Agility/Astrology** — increasingly valuable global efficiency.
8. **Firemaking 99** — worthwhile before a broad maxing push because its Skillcape gives +5% global skill XP, but not necessary before enjoying early combat.

## Session checklist

### Every login

- [ ] Harvest and replant Farming plots.
- [ ] Confirm the bank has free slots.
- [ ] Check whether a tool upgrade, Auto Eat tier, or bank slot is affordable.
- [ ] Equip the relevant potion, familiar, Astrology modifiers, and Skillcape if owned.
- [ ] Choose the next action by the current bottleneck—not simply by the highest skill.

### Before going offline

- [ ] Choose an action with enough inputs and bank space for the whole absence.
- [ ] Prefer a gathering skill if production inputs may run out.
- [ ] Never begin unattended combat without checking the exact setup in the Combat Simulator or an up-to-date idle calculation.
- [ ] Export a fresh backup before risky milestones or large changes.

## Items not to sell casually

- Ancient Ring of Skills / Ancient Ring of Mastery
- Amulet of Fishing
- Gold Topaz Ring and either half of Aorpheat's Signet Ring
- Circlet of Rhaelyx / Jewel of Rhaelyx
- Mastery tokens
- Stardust and Golden Stardust
- Diamonds and other gems needed for upgrades, potions, summoning, or crafting
- Unique dungeon drops, pets, keys, clue items, and anything marked as difficult to replace
