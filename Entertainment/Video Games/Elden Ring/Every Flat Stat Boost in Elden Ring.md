
**Nine talismans, four crystal tears, and 28 armor pieces in Elden Ring's base game directly add stat points.** No other equipment categories do this. Notable gaps: Vigor and Mind have **no dedicated heirloom talisman and no Knot Crystal Tear**, making them the hardest stats to boost with gear.

---

## Talismans that add raw stat points

Elden Ring's stat-boosting talismans fall into three families: **Soreseals** (+5 to four stats, 15% more damage taken), **Scarseals** (+3 to four stats, 10% more damage taken), and **Heirlooms** (+5 to one stat, no penalty). One outlier — Millicent's Prosthesis — adds +5 Dexterity with a successive-attack bonus. No heirloom exists for Vigor, Mind, Endurance, or Arcane in the base game.

**Two Fingers Heirloom** adds **+5 Faith with zero downside**, found in the Purified Ruins basement in southeast Liurnia (break the wooden boards hiding the entrance).

**Marika's Scarseal** adds **+3 to Mind, Intelligence, Faith, and Arcane** at the cost of 10% increased damage taken. Found in Siofra River near the waterfall northwest of Worshipper's Woods. **Marika's Soreseal** is the stronger version at **+5 to the same four stats** with 15% more damage taken, located behind an imp statue in Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree.

**Radagon's Scarseal** (+3 Vigor/Endurance/Strength/Dexterity, 10% damage penalty) drops from the Ancient Hero of Zamor in Weeping Evergaol. **Radagon's Soreseal** (+5 to the same four stats, 15% penalty) sits in Fort Faroth in Dragonbarrow, grabbable early via a suicide run with no boss kill required.

**Starscourge Heirloom** (+5 Strength) is in Fort Gael, Caelid. **Prosthesis-Wearer Heirloom** (+5 Dexterity) is a reward from Millicent's questline at the Church of the Plague. **Stargazer Heirloom** (+5 Intelligence) is in the Divine Tower of Liurnia, accessible via Ranni's quest. **Millicent's Prosthesis** (+5 Dexterity plus successive attack power) is obtained by killing Millicent.

## Knot Crystal Tears

The Wondrous Physick's stat-boosting tears — called Knot Crystal Tears — each grant **+10 to a single stat for 3 minutes**. Only Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, and Faith have one. Vigor, Mind, Endurance, and Arcane do not. All four are picked up from stone bowls in the open world with no boss fight required.

**Faith-knot Crystal Tear** (+10 Faith, 3 minutes) — Weeping Peninsula, north of the Demi-Human Forest Ruins, surrounded by Miranda Sprouts.

**Strength-knot Crystal Tear** (+10 Strength, 3 minutes) — Stormhill near Stormhill Shack, guarded by a Troll. Note: two-handing multiplies Strength by 1.5×, so 20 Strength while two-handing = effective 30 Strength.

**Dexterity-knot Crystal Tear** (+10 Dex, 3 minutes) — small island southwest of Boilprawn Shack in Liurnia.

**Intelligence-knot Crystal Tear** (+10 Int, 3 minutes) — south of Caria Manor at the bottom of a cliff, guarded by Fingercreepers.

## Bonus: Godrick's Great Rune

When activated with a Rune Arc, Godrick's Great Rune grants **+5 to every stat simultaneously** until death. Stacks with all talismans and armor above. Available after defeating Godrick and activating the rune at the Divine Tower of Limgrave. Rune Arcs are limited but farmable from rats or via co-op.

---

## Key stacking notes

- Radagon's Soreseal and Scarseal **cannot be worn together** (same for Marika's pair)
- Soreseal/Scarseal stat boosts **do not help with soft cap scaling** — they primarily help meet requirements and increase raw HP/FP pools
- **Buff-and-swap works**: equip a stat talisman to meet a weapon or spell requirement, apply the buff, then swap to a different talisman — the buff persists
- Godrick's Great Rune stacks with everything and is the single most efficient way to meet multiple stat requirements simultaneously
