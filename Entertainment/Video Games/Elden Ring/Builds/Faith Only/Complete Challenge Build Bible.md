
**See also:** [[../../Every Flat Stat Boost in Elden Ring]] — complete reference for all 9 stat-boosting talismans, 4 Knot Crystal Tears, and 28 armor pieces; useful for planning temporary requirement-meets.

**With every stat locked at 10 except Faith, this challenge build demands surgical knowledge of exactly which items work within those brutal constraints.** At Faith 44 and fresh past Margit, you have more viable options than you might expect — roughly 77 castable incantations, 8 usable Sacred Seals, a handful of zero-requirement unique weapons, and dozens of infusable weapons that become Faith-scaling powerhouses through Flame Art and Sacred affinities. The real damage comes from incantations, with melee weapons supplementing between casts. Your two biggest enemies are **78 FP** (Mind 10) and roughly **414 HP** (Vigor 10), making every talisman slot and Crystal Tear choice life-or-death.
 
This guide covers every usable item across eight categories, organized by game progression so you know exactly what to grab and when.

## 1. Weapons that actually work at 10/10/10/10
 
With Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, and Arcane all locked at 10, the vast majority of Elden Ring's faith weapons are off-limits. The Winged Scythe (16 Str/16 Dex), Blasphemous Blade (22 Str/15 Dex), Halo Scythe (13 Str/16 Dex), and Erdsteel Dagger (12 Dex) all exceed your caps. What remains falls into two categories: pure-Faith unique weapons and infusable weapons with requirements at or below 10/10.
 
### Unique Faith weapons
 
| Weapon | Requirements | Damage | Skill | Weight | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cipher Pata** | Fai 30 | Pure Holy | Unblockable Blade (pierces all shields) | **0** | Roundtable Hold — jump off upper balcony, find corpse on bed | Available now. Weightless. Two-handing dual-wields both fists. Short range limits it against some bosses. |
| **Coded Sword** | Fai 20 | Pure Holy, S scaling at +10 | Unblockable Blade (wider sweep) | **0** | Fortified Manor, 2F, Leyndell Capital | Best pure-Faith weapon in the game. **Missable** — unobtainable after killing Maliketh. |
| **Cinquedea** | Str 10, Dex 10 | Physical only | Quickstep (cannot change) | 3.5 | Cliff below Bestial Sanctum, Dragonbarrow | No Faith scaling on melee hits, but **boosts Bestial Incantation damage by ~10%** when held. Use as off-hand stat stick. Accessible very early via waygate. |
 
**The holy damage problem**: Both Cipher Pata and Coded Sword deal pure holy damage, which is heavily resisted by Radagon, Elden Beast, and several late-game bosses. You need a fire-damage alternative for those fights — hence infusable weapons with Flame Art affinity.
 
### Infusable weapons (for Sacred and Flame Art affinity)
 
These weapons have Str ≤10 and Dex ≤10, allowing them to be infused via Ashes of War. **Flame Art affinity** (fire damage + Faith scaling) is generally superior to Sacred (holy damage + Faith scaling) because fewer endgame bosses resist fire. You need the **Red-Hot Whetblade** (Redmane Castle, Caelid) for Flame Art and the **Sanctified Whetblade** (Fortified Manor, Leyndell — **missable** after Maliketh) for Sacred.

### Recommended weapon progression
 
Start with your **Club** (Flame Art infusion as soon as you get the Red-Hot Whetblade). Grab the **Hand Axe** early for faster DPS. Pick up **Cipher Pata** at Roundtable Hold once you need a weightless holy option. Rush the **Coded Sword** in Leyndell for its S-rank Faith scaling. Keep a **Flame Art Broadsword or Hand Axe** as your fire-damage alternative for holy-resistant bosses. Hold the **Cinquedea** in your off-hand when casting Bestial incantations.

**Key locations**: The **Finger Seal** costs 800 runes from Twin Maiden Husks (immediate). The **Godslayer's Seal** is in Stormveil Castle behind a Stonesword Key imp statue — **grab it now**, as it's your best seal from Faith 27 to 68. The **Erdtree Seal** is in Volcano Manor's Prison Town (mid-game) and becomes your primary once you hit Faith 69+. The **Gravel Stone Seal** drops from a Leyndell Knight in the capital and is **missable** after burning the Erdtree. The **Giant's Seal** is deep in the Giant-Conquering Hero's Grave (Mountaintops).
 
**Optimal seal loadout at your current Faith 44**: Cast with the **Godslayer's Seal** in your main hand. Hold the relevant specialty seal in your off-hand for passive boosts — Gravel Stone for lightning spells, Giant's for flame spells, Clawmark for bestial spells. Once you reach Faith 69+, swap your main casting hand to the **Erdtree Seal**.

## 4. Ashes of War for Faith scaling
 
Understanding the affinity system is critical. **Flame Art** adds fire damage and scales with Faith. **Sacred** adds holy damage and scales with Faith. **Lightning adds lightning damage but scales with Dexterity** — useless for this build as a weapon affinity, though the skills themselves still deal lightning damage. Two key items unlock these affinities on almost any Ash of War:
 
- **Red-Hot Whetblade** (Redmane Castle, Caelid) → unlocks Flame Art on any physical or fire-type Ash of War
- **Sanctified Whetblade** (Fortified Manor, Leyndell) → unlocks Sacred on any physical or golden-type Ash of War — **missable after Maliketh**
 
With both whetblades, nearly every Ash of War in the game can apply Sacred or Flame Art, giving enormous flexibility.

## 6. Crystal Tears for the Flask of Wondrous Physick
 
The Physick flask lets you combine two Crystal Tears for a custom buff. With 78 FP and 414 HP, tear selection is transformative.
 
### The essential combinations
 
**Buff-up combo for boss fights**: Mix the **Cerulean Hidden Tear** (eliminates all FP cost for ~10 seconds) with an elemental shrouding tear. Drink, then rapid-fire cast Golden Vow (47 FP) + Flame, Grant Me Strength (28 FP) + weapon buff — all for free. This single combination solves the build's FP problem for burst windows.
 
**Survival combo**: The **Opaline Bubbletear** (absorbs one hit with ~90% damage negation) paired with the **Crimson Crystal Tear** (restores 50% HP) keeps you alive through attacks that would otherwise one-shot.
 
### Full Crystal Tear list
 
| Crystal Tear | Effect | Source | When |

### Essential NPCs and their questlines
 
**Gurranq, Beast Clergyman** at the Bestial Sanctum is reachable immediately via the waygate behind the Third Church of Marika. Feed him Deathroot (first one from Tibia Mariner in Summonwater Village or Deathtouched Catacombs) to receive the Clawmark Seal, then Bestial Sling, Bestial Vitality, Beast Claw, Stone of Gurranq, Gurranq's Beast Claw, and ultimately an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone across 9 Deathroots. **He goes hostile after the 4th Deathroot** — deal roughly 1,000 damage to calm him, then continue feeding.
 
**Brother Corhyn** at Roundtable Hold sells basic incantations (Catch Flame, Flame Sling, Heal, Rejection, and Fortification spells). He moves to Altus Plateau after Rennala or the Grand Lift, then progresses through his Goldmask questline. His questline ultimately requires casting Law of Regression (37 Int), which **this build cannot do** without a Larval Tear respec.
 
**D, Hunter of the Dead** sells Order's Blade (Int 13 required) and Litany of Proper Death (Int 17 required) — **both are unusable** for this build since Int is locked at 10. His questline is still relevant for connecting you to Gurranq.
 
**Gowry** south of Sellia sells **Pest Threads** (7,500 runes) after progressing Millicent's quest. Pest Threads is one of the best boss-killing incantations in the game at only 11 Faith and 19 FP. Killing Gowry after completing Millicent's full questline drops the **Flock's Canvas Talisman** (+8% incantation damage).
 
**Gideon Ofnir** at Roundtable Hold gives free incantations as you explore: **Black Flame's Protection** after reporting on the Godskin, and **Lord's Divine Fortification** after defeating Malenia.

### Immediate action plan from Stormveil
 
Your priority sequence right now: clear Stormveil Castle and **grab the Godskin Prayerbook** behind the Stonesword Key imp statue near the cellar. After defeating Godrick, push into Liurnia. Visit **Miriel at the Church of Vows** and give him the Godskin Prayerbook to buy Black Flame and Black Flame Blade. Kill the **Leyndell Knight near Artist's Shack** for the Dragon Cult Prayerbook and bring that to Miriel for Lightning Spear, Honed Bolt, and Electrify Armament. Grab the **Two Fingers Heirloom** from Purified Ruins for +5 Faith. Detour to Caelid via waygate to reach **Gurranq** for Bestial Sling, and pick up **Flame, Grant Me Strength** behind Fort Gael. With the Godslayer's Seal from Stormveil, Black Flame, Lightning Spear, and Flame Grant Me Strength all in hand, you'll have a complete offensive toolkit to carry you through the mid-game.
 
## 9. What opens up with stat-boosting equipment
 
At base 10 in every non-Faith stat, a large portion of the game's weapons and incantations are locked. The stat-boosting gear from sections 5–7 can push those locked stats high enough to meet requirements — either permanently via talismans and armor, or temporarily via crystal tears and Godrick's Great Rune. This section maps exactly what becomes available at each boosted threshold.
 
All figures assume base stat of 10. Items are cumulative — e.g. "Radagon's Soreseal" pushes Vigor to 15, and stacking the Crimson Hood on top gives 16.

### Stacking combinations for key unlocks
 
Some of the most powerful unlocks require meeting two stat requirements simultaneously. Here are the most impactful multi-stat combinations:
 
**Halo Scythe** (Str 13, Dex 16, Faith 15 — native holy Faith weapon with Sacred Blade skill):
- Radagon's Soreseal (+5 Str → 15, +5 Dex → 15) + Okina Mask (+3 Dex → 18) = Str 15 ✅ Dex 18 ✅. Faith met by leveling. Achievable mid-game.
 
**Golden Order Seal** (Int 17, Faith 17 — best hybrid seal):
- Marika's Soreseal (+5 Int → 15) + Twinsage Crown or any +2 Int crown (+2 → 17) = Int 17 ✅. Faith 17 easily met by leveling. Requires very late game for Soreseal.
- Alternatively: Stargazer Heirloom (+5 Int → 15) + Queen's Crescent Crown (+3 → 18) = Int 18 ✅. Mid-late game.
 
**Order's Blade** (Int 13, Faith 13 — holy weapon buff from D Hunter of the Dead):
- Marika's Scarseal alone (+3 Int → 13, +3 Faith → 13) = both requirements met simultaneously. Early-mid game. Faith 13 met by the scarseal even at base Faith 10.
 
**Greyoll's Roar** (Arc 15, Faith 28 — −20% enemy attack debuff):
- Marika's Soreseal (+5 Arc → 15) + Faith leveled to 28 = fully castable. Mid-game once you hit Faith 28.
- Silver Tear Mask (+8 Arc → 18) + Marika's Scarseal (+3 Arc → but doesn't stack with Soreseal) — just the mask alone at 18 Arc exceeds the 15 requirement. Accessible post-Radahn.
 
**Uchigatana with Flame Art** (Str 11, Dex 15 — fast Faith-scaling katana):
- Radagon's Soreseal (+5 Str → 15, +5 Dex → 15) = both met. Available as early as Fort Faroth (Caelid). Uchigatana found at Deathtouched Catacombs or bought from Twin Maiden Husks.
 
**Erdsteel Dagger** (Dex 12 — native Faith-scaling dagger):
- Any +2 Dex armor (Lazuli Crown or Imp Head Long-Tongued) = Dex 12 ✅. Extremely low barrier. Native B Faith scaling and holy damage, critical rating 110.

## Conclusion
 
This challenge build is far more viable than it first appears. The **77 usable incantations** provide a deep toolbox covering fire, lightning, holy, and black flame damage types, letting you exploit boss weaknesses throughout the game. The FP bottleneck (78 FP) is your biggest constraint, but the **Cerulean Hidden Tear** transforms it from crippling to manageable by enabling free buff stacking before boss fights. Flame Art-infused weapons like the Hand Axe and Broadsword give you reliable melee when FP runs dry, and the weightless Cipher Pata and Coded Sword serve as your pure-Faith damage sticks. The Erdtree Seal at Faith 69+ combined with specialized off-hand seals (Godslayer's for black flame, Gravel Stone for lightning, Giant's for fire) maximizes every incantation school. Survival at 10 Vigor hinges on never getting hit, making Radagon's Soreseal, the Opaline Bubbletear, and Dragoncrest Shield Talismans non-negotiable. The build rewards precise play, smart buff management, and knowing exactly which 4 talismans and 2 Crystal Tears each boss fight demands.
