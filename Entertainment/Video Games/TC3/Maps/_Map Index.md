## How map type drives strategy (quick rules)
- **Crystal-poor maps (Mansion):** income ceiling is low — value every crystal, lean nuclear-on-super, shorter games.
- **Crystal-rich maps (Golf Course):** snowball econ hard; you can afford Space Link / Mothership tech.
- **Oil-heavy naval (Middle-East):** build **oil before power** (better ROI past 3 rigs); Naval Shipyard early.
- **Naval/island:** transports (Hovercraft/Transport Ship) mandatory; AA matters (planes cross water); see [[Strategy - 30-Minute Enemy-Island Rush]].
- **Land/Pangaea:** no docks to strand; rush enemy CCs directly (after the 20:00 window).
