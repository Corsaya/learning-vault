## Core doctrine — "it should be called *Economy* 3"
Mike's entire thesis: **economy wins games.** Repeated ~6 times. Everything else is secondary.

### Opening
- **Teleport base before the map even loads** for the fastest possible start.
- **FFA → rush middle** for the **mega crystal** (= supercrystal in reference: 2x income).
- **Plant at your own base FIRST** (power or nuclear) before contesting middle.
- Power vs Nuclear at start: power plant if unsure/competitive; nuclear if confident in a long game / already have a mega crystal at base. (Mirrors reference: nuclear = better long-term, power = safer when capital scarce.)

### Don'ts (beginner traps)
- **Don't buy a Tank Factory at spawn** — drains econ, leaves you at ~$50 with no income engine. Build economy first.
- **Don't spam one unit type** — mix Heavy/Light Soldiers with Anti-Air Soldiers, Snipers, Juggernauts, Stealth Bombers, Space Fighters.

### Mid/late doctrine
- **Research unit-cap houses** (Soldier/Tank/Plane/Naval House) — many beginners don't know caps are raisable. Going 6→8 Space Fighters is a decisive swing.
- **Attack the enemy ECONOMY, never the base first.** A base with no income makes nothing. Kill income → they're frozen → base falls on its own.
- **Build-denial:** drop your own power plant on a contested crystal while attacking so they can't rebuild turrets/bunkers there (can't build near enemy buildings). *(Same trick PI's guide teaches.)*
- **Micro:** pull damaged units out of a fight, rotate fresh ones in.
- **Team roles:** always have one player *countering* what the enemy does; don't all-attack or all-defend. Coordinate so you don't double-buy (he's seen "4 barracks on one team").
- **Defend your mega crystals above all** — lose your economy and there's no comeback.
