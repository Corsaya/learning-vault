## The 7 Steps
1. **Grow economy** — power plants on every home-island crystal, expand to neighbor islands. Target **80–120 CPM**. Spend first **8–10 min** building econ.
2. **Build a Barracks on the ENEMY's island + build an army** — the core trick. Kill their crystal's plants, plant your own power plant there (denies their rebuild), then drop a Barracks in their face. Spam **Heavy Soldiers** + a couple **Snipers**.
3. **Stop opponent growth** — attack their docks so they're trapped on their home island.
4. **Isolate** — with docks gone they can't transport; you roam freely building more econ.
5. **Grow army again, now with tanks** — Light Tanks or Explosive Tanks via a Tank Factory.
6. **Destroy Command Centers** — only after the 20-min mark (see new mechanic below). Tanks in first as damage-soakers, infantry following ("blitzkrieg").
7. **Clean up** — planes (or Space Fighters if rich) to mop up oil rigs/stragglers. Wait for **25:00** before the final kill to claim the free skin.

## Economy doctrine (ROI numbers he cites)
- **Power Plant ROI ≈ 6.5 min** → reference says **6:40** break-even (regular crystal). ✅ Effectively confirmed; reference value is the precise one.
- **Nuclear Plant ROI ≈ 9.3 min** (his number) → reference says **~8:40** (regular crystal). ⚠️ **Discrepancy — verify.** (May differ on FFA vs standard pricing.)
- **Oil ROI at 3 rigs ≈ 6.17 min**, beating power plants — so on maps with **>3 oil spots, build oil before power**. His math = (Shipyard $80 + 3×Oil Ship $35) / +$30 CPM = $185/30 = **6.17**. ✅ This independently validates the reference's **Naval Shipyard $80** and **Oil Ship $35** prices.
- ROI keeps improving with more rigs (he cites ~10 rigs on Middle-East map).

## Unit doctrine (all checks ✅ vs reference)
- **Heavy Soldier > Light Soldier** as the workhorse: both 100 HP, Heavy does 2 DPS + shorter reload vs Light's 1 DPS. Light = meat shield only.
- **Snipers** hard-counter cornered infantry (7.5 DPS, range 23).
- **3–4 Explosive Tanks kill a CC**: Explosive Tank = 120 contact dmg; CC = 450 HP → 4 needed. ✅
- **Light Tanks** preferred over Heavy for CC dives — their job is to *soak* damage (both 275 HP), not deal it.
- **"Navy loses to air"** — his stance; he kills enemy oil rigs with planes, not boats.

## ⚠️ NEW MECHANICS — not in TC3_Reference.md (candidate additions)
1. **Command Centers are buffed/near-invulnerable for the first 20:00 of a match** — only **Artillery** can damage them before then. This is why step 6 waits for the 20-min mark. **The reference's Command Center entry (line ~205) says nothing about this 20-minute invulnerability window — it should.**
2. **Free skin reward** for completing/finishing a map — he intentionally waits until the **25:00 mark** before destroying the last enemy building to guarantee the skin drops. Not mentioned anywhere in the reference.
3. **Build-denial via your own plant:** placing your power plant on a contested crystal blocks the enemy from turret/bunker/AA spamming there (can't build near enemy buildings). Reference *states* the no-build-near-enemy rule (line 45) but doesn't frame it as this offensive tool.
