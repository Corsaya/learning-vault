# Strategy — Economy-First Doctrine (the spine)

> **Type:** Universal doctrine, map-agnostic. **Confidence:** High — every credible source independently teaches it.
> **Sources:** [[Guide - Beginner Tips (Mike, 2019)]] · [[Guide - Win in 30 Minutes Rush (PI)]] · [[Guide - Basics Tutorial (2021)]] · observed in [[Match - woof1977 2v2 naval (URiNtrLAfcc)]]
> All prices = TC3_Reference.md (2026), standard pricing. FFA/1v1 = ×0.75.

## The one rule
**Income wins. Spend the first 8–10 minutes building economy before committing to an army.** Attack the enemy's *economy*, never their base first — a base with no income produces nothing.

## Build priority (opening → mid)
1. **Plant at your own base/crystal first** (Power $65 = +$10 CPM regular, or Nuclear $130 = +$15 regular / +$30 super).
2. **Occupy every reachable crystal** — an idle crystal is $10–30 CPM wasted.
3. **Oil before power when a map has >3 oil spots** — at 3 rigs, oil ROI ≈ 6.17 min beats Power's 6:40 (per PI). Math: (Shipyard $80 + 3×Oil Ship $35) / +$30 CPM.
4. **Nuclear-on-supercrystal** is the long-game engine (+$30 CPM, break-even ~4:20). Grab supers early.
5. Only *then* production buildings. **Never buy a Tank Factory ($235) at spawn** — it strangles your economy.

| Power Plant | $65 | +$10 (reg) / +$20 (super) | 6:40 / 3:15 |

## Combat application of the doctrine
- **Build-denial:** when attacking a contested crystal, place YOUR power plant on it — enemy can't build turrets/bunkers/AA near your building. Locks the spot.
- **Kill order vs an opponent:** income buildings → docks/transports (strand them on their island) → then army/base.
- **Defense:** guard your supercrystals above all. Lose your economy = no comeback.
- **Micro:** rotate damaged units out of fights; don't feed the same wounded unit.
- **Army comp:** mix unit types (don't mono-Heavy-Soldier). Snipers counter massed infantry; AA Soldiers/Turrets ($35) counter air.

## Team coordination
- One player *counters* the enemy's moves; the other pressures. Never all-defense or all-offense.
- Talk so you don't double-buy (no "4 barracks on one team").
- Losing your Command Center disables use of allies' production — protect it.
