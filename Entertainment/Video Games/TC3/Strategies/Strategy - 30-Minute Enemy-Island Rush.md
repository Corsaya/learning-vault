# Strategy — 30-Minute Enemy-Island Rush

> **Type:** Win-condition strategy. **Best maps:** island/naval (also Pangaea by enemy CCs). **Vs:** casuals/randoms — NOT strong players.
> **Source:** [[Guide - Win in 30 Minutes Rush (PI)]] (2020). **Doctrine base:** [[Strategy - Economy-First Doctrine]].
> Prices = TC3_Reference.md (2026), standard. Verify before use.

## The idea
Out-tempo a weaker opponent: build a fast economy, then **plant a Barracks ON the enemy's island**, spam Heavy Soldiers + Snipers in their face, strand them by killing their docks, and crack their Command Center the instant the 20-minute CC-invulnerability window ends.

## 7 steps (PI)
1. **Economy** — power plants on every home crystal, expand outward. Target **80–120 CPM**. First **8–10 min**.
2. **Barracks on enemy island + army** — kill their crystal's plants, plant your own (build-denial), drop a Barracks. Spam **Heavy Soldiers** + a couple **Snipers**. Add Soldier House + Research Center to buff infantry.
3. **Stop their growth** — destroy their **docks** so they can't transport off their island.
4. **Isolate** — they're trapped; you roam and out-econ them further.
5. **Grow army again w/ tanks** — Tank Factory → **Light Tanks** (damage soakers) or **Explosive Tanks**.
6. **Destroy Command Centers** — only **after 20:00** (see mechanic). Tanks in first, infantry following ("blitzkrieg"). **3–4 Explosive Tanks** kill a CC (120 dmg each vs 450 HP).
7. **Clean up** — planes (or Space Fighters if rich) mop up oil rigs/stragglers. **Wait for 25:00** before the final kill to guarantee the free skin.

## Key mechanics this relies on
- ⚠️ **CC 20-minute invulnerability** — Command Centers are near-unkillable for the first 20:00 (only Artillery scratches them). *Not yet in the reference — pending add.* This is WHY the timeline waits for 20:00.
- **Build-denial** — your plant on their crystal blocks their turret/bunker rebuild.
- **Heavy Soldier** = most cost-efficient workhorse ($20, 100 HP, 2 DPS). **Light Tank** dives as a damage soak (275 HP).
- **Kill economy/docks, not the base**, until you're ready for the timed CC push.

## When NOT to use
Strong/competitive opponents will out-speed you and counter the enemy-island Barracks (snipers, explosive tanks, or putting you on defense). PI says it again: casuals only.
