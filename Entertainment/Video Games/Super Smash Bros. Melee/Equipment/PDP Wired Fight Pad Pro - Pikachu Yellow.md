## Current use

This is the controller being used for UnclePunch practice, Slippi unranked games, and the initial recorded gameplay baseline. Reviews made before the OEM controller arrives should name this controller so execution issues can be interpreted in context.

## Important differences from an OEM GameCube controller

- The shoulder buttons are digital rather than the original GameCube controller's analog-plus-digital triggers.
- Normal shielding, wavedashing, L-canceling, and teching remain available.
- Native analog light-shield control is unavailable.
- Trigger travel, stick feel, and button feel may differ enough that some muscle memory will need adjustment after switching to the OEM DOL-003.

These differences do not prevent productive beginner practice. Current priorities—movement, deliberate attacks, recovery, DI, teching, and decision-making—still transfer.
