## [[Summary]]

The strongest trait across both characters is active, persistent offense. The largest shared leak is continuing forward after the safe portion of an interaction has ended, which gives Marth opportunities to reverse. Falco benefits from more laser-led neutral and pressure resets; Jigglypuff benefits from aerial feints and attacking Marth after he commits.

## What is already working

- Comfortable pursuing opponents toward the edge.
- Maintains composure and remains threatening at high percent.
- Converts momentum into meaningful damage and stocks.
- Jigglypuff adapted after early losses and assembled a strong comeback sequence.

## Recurring situations

### Neutral

- Falco approaches Marth directly too often instead of using lasers to force a jump, shield, or approach.
- Jigglypuff sometimes enters horizontally at sword height rather than drifting around the edge of Marth's range.
- Both characters would benefit from more feints and fewer immediate commitments.

### Punish game

- Good instinct to continue advantage, but extensions sometimes continue after the guaranteed sequence ends.
- Use `hit -> reposition -> punish the escape` instead of automatically attacking again.

### Defense and disadvantage

- Avoid forcing an immediate answer after being hit; reset position when the follow-up is no longer safe.
- With Jigglypuff, preserve aerial resources and vary drift while descending.

### Recovery and ledge

- Falco should mix recovery height, shorten distance, and timing.
- Jigglypuff should exploit multiple jumps to threaten reversals without drifting into Marth's forward-air lane.

### Execution and inputs

- Make Falco's short-hop laser and retreating aerials automatic.
- Practice Jigglypuff fast-fall timing and delayed back-air spacing.

## Three priorities

1. Falco: use laser or movement to create the opening before approaching.
2. Jigglypuff: bait the sword swing, then punish the ending position with back-air.
3. After the first hit, pause and cover the opponent's escape instead of always extending immediately.

## UnclePunch drills

- Falco short-hop laser consistency while moving forward and backward.
- Falco recovery mixups: high angle, low angle, shortened side-B, and delayed timing.
- Jigglypuff retreating and delayed back-air spacing against Marth.

## Match goals for the next session

- Jigglypuff: do not approach Marth directly at sword height; make him swing first.
- Both: after landing one hit, consciously decide between extending and resetting.
