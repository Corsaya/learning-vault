# Melee Gameplay Reviews

This section turns gameplay footage into a small number of concrete practice targets.

## What to submit

Either of these works:

- A gameplay video, preferably 60 fps
- A Slippi `.slp` replay, ideally accompanied by video if visual presentation matters

Include the player character, opponent character, which player/port is yours, controller used, and anything that felt difficult. Short clips are useful for one situation; one to three complete games are better for identifying habits.

## Review priorities

1. The largest repeated problem affecting stocks
2. Neutral decisions and approach habits
3. Punish-game openings and dropped follow-ups
4. Defense: DI, teching, corner choices, and landing
5. Recovery and edgeguarding
6. Execution issues visible from gameplay or the input overlay

Each review should finish with no more than three priorities and matching UnclePunch drills. This keeps practice actionable.
