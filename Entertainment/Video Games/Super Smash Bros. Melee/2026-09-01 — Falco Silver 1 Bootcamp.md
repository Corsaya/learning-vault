# Falco Silver 1 Bootcamp

## Target and standard

**Silver 1 is the target, not a promised rank.** Online matchmaking produces opponents, connection quality, and variance that a schedule cannot control. The controllable job is 4 hours/week of deliberate Falco work, replay review, and ranked exposure. If the rank lags while the gates below are met, keep training the leak rather than swapping mains or declaring the system broken.

The standing main decision is Falco for 30 days minimum. No character rotation during the bootcamp.

## Weekly calendar — beginning September 21

| When | Block | Minutes |
|---|---|---:|
| Monday 3:45–4:30 PM | UnclePunch execution + movement | 45 |
| Wednesday after dinner | Unranked/ranked focus session | 45 |
| Friday 3:45–4:30 PM | UnclePunch scenario + 3–5 games | 45 |
| Saturday 2:00–3:00 PM | Ranked set block + review one loss | 60 |
| Sunday 10:45–11:30 AM | Ranked set block + review one loss | 45 |

**Total: 240 minutes / 4 hours weekly.** If schoolwork displaces a session, drop the Sunday ranked block first; keep one execution session and one replay review every week.

## Session structure

1. **10 min — warm-up:** dash dance, short hop, fast fall, L-cancel, wavedash.
2. **15 min — one drill:** never rotate through five drills in one session.
3. **20–30 min — matches:** one explicit match rule tied to the drill.
4. **5 min — evidence:** save one replay; write one execution miss and one decision error.

## 12-week progression

| Weeks | Focus | Match rule | Gate to progress |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | OEM feel; short hop, L-cancel, dash dance, wavedash | No panic roll; reset to center after hitstun | 8/10 short hops per side; 16/20 clean L-cancel reps |
| 3–4 | Short-hop laser, retreating aerial, teching | Every approach begins with laser, dash dance, or a bait | 8/10 controlled low lasers in stationary, forward, and retreating reps |
| 5–6 | Recovery, DI, and getting off ledge | Escape first; do not mash an attack after being hit | Identify one alternate recovery route in every reviewed stock loss |
| 7–8 | Neutral and anti-approach | Win center; make them swing before committing | Fewer than three blind aerial approaches per game in two reviewed games |
| 9–10 | Simple punish and edgeguard | `hit -> reposition -> punish escape` | Convert one clean opener into a deliberate follow-up in each game |
| 11–12 | Ranked volume and adaptation | Carry one matchup lesson into the next set | Review four losses; name the repeated situation, not "I got outplayed" |

## Ranked protocol

- Play ranked only after the 25-minute warm-up/drill block.
- Stop after **three tilted losses** or **90 minutes total**; save the replay instead of queueing angry.
- First 10 ranked sets: data only. Do not use rank movement as the verdict.
- From week 5 onward, target 10–15 ranked games/week.
- Silver attempt window begins after week 12 only if the execution gates are mostly met. If not, repeat the relevant two-week block.

## Things deliberately excluded

No multi-shine, ledgedash grind, shield drops, or tech-chasing tree optimization. They are not required to become a competent low-level Falco and will eat the time needed for movement, neutral, recovery, and analysis.
