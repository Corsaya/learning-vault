# Entertainment

Books, movies, music, TV, video games. Three modes per item — pick what fits:

- **Log** — just the title + date consumed. Lightweight.
- **Review** — short take, rating, thoughts.
- **Deep notes** — full analysis, characters, themes (e.g., One Piece's `01 - Favorites/`).

No rule says everything needs the same depth. Most items are fine as a log line; a few earn the deep dive.
