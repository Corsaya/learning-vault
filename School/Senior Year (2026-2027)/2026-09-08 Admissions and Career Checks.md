# Admissions and career checks

Personal scores, income and preferences are held in the private life/finance notes. This public note records source checks and a reusable action checklist. Historical chat recommendations are not current policy.

[ERAU first-time admissions](https://erau.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/first-time-students), checked September 8, 2026. Confirm the intended campus, major and entry term in the application portal.

## Decision order

1. Record the actual latest score report, transcript scale and current coursework privately. Do not convert a school-specific weighted GPA into another school's scale by guesswork.
2. Finish the personal essay from real experiences; review for specificity and reflection, not a manufactured inspirational arc.
3. Compare intended major, campus fit and family-approved affordability. Maintain at least one acceptable affordable option; do not label a school a safety solely from a general acceptance rate.
4. Choose ED only if binding attendance is genuinely the preferred, affordable decision. A higher aggregate ED admit rate does not establish the causal benefit for a particular applicant.
5. Work backward from the chosen application **and materials** deadlines. Requests for records and recommendations need lead time.

These steps are a proposed review workflow, not permission to submit applications, contact schools or commit to ED.

## FAA and other summer internships

The official [FAA internship overview](https://www.faa.gov/jobs/students/internships) confirms an **unpaid** Volunteer Service Program for eligible enrolled students and lists Gateways student/recent-graduate pathways. It does not establish a paid summer-2027 Atlantic City opening, salary, application window or eligibility for a high-school graduate about to start college. Some sections retain old dates. The older MSI link did not resolve in this check. Do not promote a historical assistant's confident eligibility/pay statements into a plan.

Before treating any role as viable, record its exact official vacancy URL, closing date, location, age rule, student-status rule, citizenship/work-authorization rule, pay, required start/end dates and required documents. A general program description cannot substitute for the vacancy. Application season and available work dates are different constraints.

## Resume evidence sheet

| Field | Fill from evidence |
|---|---|
| Education | School, expected graduation, GPA and scale, verified relevant courses |
| Technical skills | Language/tool plus something built and debugged independently |
| Project | Problem; specific owned contribution; repository/demo; test or measured result |
| Employment | Employer/title/dates; concrete responsibilities; documented impact |
| Athletics/leadership | Role, dated result and verifiable responsibility |
| Availability | Start/end dates; mandatory school/crew commitments; transportation |

Keep contact details in private copies. Do not invent users, revenue, independent coding ability, leadership titles or internship acceptance odds. A project that can be explained and debugged is better evidence than a large generated codebase with no ownership clarity.
