# Summer Reading Requirements

## Assigned books

- *The Kite Runner*
- *Night*
- *A Raisin in the Sun* (selected instead of *In Cold Blood*)

During the second full week of school, each book may receive an essay test, an objective test, or both. Answers must demonstrate knowledge gained from reading the work itself.

## Criteria to capture while reading

- Significance of the title
- Main characters and their roles in developing the plot
- Character traits and flaws
- Significant events and plot development
- Important characters, facts, and details
- Themes and symbols developed through the characters and events
- Personal reflections, connections, and assessment
- Specific questions about confusing passages
- Confusing passages recorded with page numbers
- Important passages or details recorded while reading

## Method

Use each book's reading log during every session. Move durable observations into its character chart and plot/theme/symbol document. Complete the assessment-prep sheet after finishing the book; do not wait until test week to reconstruct the details.
