# Vectors vs. Scalars

**1) A runner running for 3 hours**

SCALAR — time has magnitude only, no direction

**2) A gas tank containing 20 gallons**

SCALAR — volume has no direction

**3) The work a crane does lifting a container 50 feet**

SCALAR — work is a scalar quantity in physics

**4) A pitcher throwing a ball with a 65 N force**

VECTOR — force has both magnitude (65 N) and a direction (toward the catcher)

**5) A car traveling with a speed of 45 mph**

SCALAR — speed alone has no direction (it would be velocity if a direction were given)

**6) A storm with winds blowing 120 mph Northeast**

VECTOR — this is a velocity: magnitude (120 mph) and direction (Northeast)

**7) The freezing point of water at 32°F**

SCALAR — temperature has no direction

**8) The magnetic field pulling a magnet toward a door**

VECTOR — a force with a specific direction

# Drawing Vectors to Scale

*These items ask you to draw the vectors by hand to scale; the drawing itself isn't something I can do for you, but here's the key data you need for each sketch so you can lay out your grid correctly (arrow lengths proportional to magnitude, correct compass direction):*

**Draw: 10 m North, 5 m West, 20 m South, 15 m East**

Lengths should be proportional: 20 m (longest) \> 15 m \> 10 m \> 5 m (shortest)

As a bonus, the net (resultant) displacement if you combine all four: net E/W = 15−5 = 10 m East, net N/S = 10−20 = 10 m South

Resultant magnitude = √(10²+10²) = 14.14 m, direction = 45° South of East

**Pairs 1–8 (draw each pair to scale, proper direction)**

1) 10 mi East & 30 mi West — opposite directions; net = 20 mi West

2) 6 m/s North & 12 m/s South — opposite directions; net = 6 m/s South

3) 4 gallons & 20 gallons — these are scalars (no direction to draw as vectors); their sum is just 24 gallons

4) 15 N East & 20 N East — same direction; net = 35 N East

5) 2 mph East & 10 mph South — perpendicular; resultant = √(2²+10²) ≈ 10.20 mph, at 78.7° South of East

6) 9 N North & 9 N South — opposite, equal magnitude; net = 0 (they cancel)

7) 1500 m West & 1000 m South — perpendicular; resultant = √(1500²+1000²) ≈ 1802.8 m, at 33.7° South of West

8) 7 mph & 28 mph — no directions are given for these two, so they can't be vector-added; treated as plain numbers they'd total 35 mph

*These items show unlabeled arrows to be added head-to-tail by hand (no numeric magnitudes given in the scan), so there's no calculation to check — just the method: slide the tail of the second vector to the head of the first without changing its length or direction, then draw the resultant from the very first tail to the very last head. # Vector Components (draw + label as ±i, ±j)

*North/East = positive; South/West = negative.*

**1) 5 m north and 10 m west**

Components: 0i + 5j  and  −10i + 0j

Resultant: R = −10i + 5j,  |R| = √(10²+5²) = √125 ≈ 11.18 m

**2) 4 miles east and 6 miles north**

Components: 4i + 0j  and  0i + 6j

Resultant: R = 4i + 6j,  |R| = √(16+36) = √52 ≈ 7.21 mi

**3) 20 miles south and 5 miles east**

Components: 0i − 20j  and  5i + 0j

Resultant: R = 5i − 20j,  |R| = √(25+400) = √425 ≈ 20.62 mi

**4) 12 mph west and 36 mph north**

Components: −12i + 0j  and  0i + 36j

Resultant: R = −12i + 36j,  |R| = √(144+1296) ≈ 37.95 mph

**5) 40 N south and 20 N west**

Components: 0i − 40j  and  −20i + 0j

Resultant: R = −20i − 40j,  |R| = √(400+1600) ≈ 44.72 N

**6) 30 feet east and 100 feet north**

Components: 30i + 0j  and  0i + 100j

Resultant: R = 30i + 100j,  |R| = √(900+10000) ≈ 104.40 ft

# Adding, Subtracting, and Multiplying with Vector Components

*Using A = 2i+7j, B = −4i+9j, C = 3i+6j, D = −8i−1j:*

**1) A + B**

(2−4)i + (7+9)j = −2i + 16j

**2) C + A**

(3+2)i + (6+7)j = 5i + 13j

**3) B − D**

(−4−(−8))i + (9−(−1))j = 4i + 10j

**4) D − A**

(−8−2)i + (−1−7)j = −10i − 8j

**5) 4D**

4(−8)i + 4(−1)j = −32i − 4j

**6) −2C**

−2(3)i + −2(6)j = −6i − 12j

**7) 7B − 2A**

7(−4i+9j) − 2(2i+7j) = (−28i+63j) − (4i+14j) = −32i + 49j

**8) 3A + 5C**

3(2i+7j) + 5(3i+6j) = (6i+21j)+(15i+30j) = 21i + 51j

# Vector Magnitude & Sin/Cos/Tan Practice

*Method: if given components, magnitude = √(x²+y²) and angle θ = tan⁻¹(|opposite|/|adjacent|) measured from the x-axis, named North/South of East/West by the signs of the components. If given a magnitude and angle, the adjacent side (x-component) = Hyp·cosθ and the opposite side (y-component) = Hyp·sinθ.*

**1) 4i and 5j**

|A| = √(4²+5²) = √41 ≈ 6.40

θ = tan⁻¹(5/4) ≈ 51.3°  →  direction: 51.3° North of East

**2) Vector of magnitude 20 at 40° (measured from the vertical reference line), pointing up and to the right**

x-component = 20·sin(40°) ≈ 12.86  (East, +)

y-component = 20·cos(40°) ≈ 15.32  (North, +)

So the vector ≈ 12.86i + 15.32j

**3) Magnitude 12 at 30° from horizontal, pointing down and to the left**

x-component = −12·cos(30°) ≈ −10.39  (West)

y-component = −12·sin(30°) = −6.00  (South)

Vector ≈ −10.39i − 6.00j,  i.e. 12 units at 30° South of West

**4) −6i and 3j**

|A| = √(36+9) = √45 ≈ 6.71

θ = tan⁻¹(3/6) ≈ 26.6°  →  direction: 26.6° North of West

**5) Magnitude 15 at 80° from horizontal, pointing down and to the left**

x-component = −15·cos(80°) ≈ −2.60  (West)

y-component = −15·sin(80°) ≈ −14.77  (South)

Vector ≈ −2.60i − 14.77j,  i.e. 15 units at 80° South of West

**6) −1i and −7j**

|A| = √(1+49) = √50 ≈ 7.07

θ = tan⁻¹(7/1) ≈ 81.9°  →  direction: 81.9° South of West

**7) 9i and −2j**

|A| = √(81+4) = √85 ≈ 9.22

θ = tan⁻¹(2/9) ≈ 12.5°  →  direction: 12.5° South of East

**8) Magnitude 5 at 50° from horizontal, pointing up and to the left**

x-component = −5·cos(50°) ≈ −3.21  (West)

y-component = 5·sin(50°) ≈ 3.83  (North)

Vector ≈ −3.21i + 3.83j,  i.e. 5 units at 50° North of West

**9) 3i and 8j**

|A| = √(9+64) = √73 ≈ 8.54

θ = tan⁻¹(8/3) ≈ 69.4°  →  direction: 69.4° North of East

# Multi-Vector Sums (final problems)

**10) 10 m due North, 6 m 40° N of W, 15 m 20° S of E, and 5 m 80° S of W**

v1 = 0i + 10j

v2 = −6cos40° i + 6sin40° j ≈ −4.60i + 3.86j

v3 = 15cos20° i − 15sin20° j ≈ 14.10i − 5.13j

v4 = −5cos80° i − 5sin80° j ≈ −0.87i − 4.92j

Resultant: R ≈ (0−4.60+14.10−0.87)i + (10+3.86−5.13−4.92)j ≈ 8.63i + 3.80j

|R| = √(8.63²+3.80²) ≈ 9.43 m,  θ = tan⁻¹(3.80/8.63) ≈ 23.8° North of East

**11) 15 mi North, 10 mi 30° S of W, 4 mi 75° N of E**

w1 = 0i + 15j

w2 = −10cos30° i − 10sin30° j ≈ −8.66i − 5.00j

w3 = 4cos75° i + 4sin75° j ≈ 1.04i + 3.86j

Resultant: R ≈ (0−8.66+1.04)i + (15−5.00+3.86)j ≈ −7.62i + 13.86j

|R| = √(7.62²+13.86²) ≈ 15.82 mi,  θ = tan⁻¹(13.86/7.62) ≈ 61.2° North of West

**12) 100 ft E, 20 ft S, 45 ft W, 20 ft N, 35 ft 45° N of W, 75 ft 60° S of E**

u1 = 100i,  u2 = −20j,  u3 = −45i,  u4 = 20j

u5 = −35cos45° i + 35sin45° j ≈ −24.75i + 24.75j

u6 = 75cos60° i − 75sin60° j ≈ 37.50i − 64.95j

Resultant: R ≈ (100−45−24.75+37.50)i + (−20+20+24.75−64.95)j ≈ 67.75i − 40.20j

|R| = √(67.75²+40.20²) ≈ 78.78 ft,  θ = tan⁻¹(40.20/67.75) ≈ 30.7° South of East
