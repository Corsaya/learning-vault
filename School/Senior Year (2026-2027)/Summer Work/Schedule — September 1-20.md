## Reading-note limit

Spend no more than 10–15 minutes updating notes after a reading session. Record only:

- new characters or meaningful character changes;
- major events;
- one important or confusing passage with its page number;
- one theme or symbol observation;
- one genuine personal reaction or question.

The templates support the reading and test preparation; they are not a second English assignment.
