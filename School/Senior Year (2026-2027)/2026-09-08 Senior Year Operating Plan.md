# Senior Year Operating Plan — Fall 2026

This is a proposed operating schedule built from Donovan's September 8 interview.
It does not replace the teacher's assignment list, the coach's training plan, or
the employer's actual roster. Donovan owns schedule decisions; this document
makes the current trade-offs visible.

## Outcomes, in order

1. Submit correct, competitive college applications and financial-aid forms.
2. Protect sleep, coursework, and crew execution toward a top-three SRAA finish.
3. Keep reliable paid work.
4. Run one bounded content experiment and improve at Melee without turning either
   into late-night debt.

## Weekly operating skeleton through November 15

### Monday — primary self-directed day

- 2:30–4:00: SAT through October 2; college applications October 5–November 15.
- 4:15–5:15: coach/program-compatible gym or recovery decision window.
- 6:15–7:45: AP Calculus/AP Physics and next-due schoolwork.
- 8:00–9:30: content production deep work.
- 9:30 onward: pack for Tuesday and wind down.

### Tuesday — crew plus minimum viable evening

- 2:30–5:30: crew.
- 6:30–8:30: next-due schoolwork.
- 8:30–9:15: deliberate Melee practice.
- 9:15–9:45: application micro-task only if one is explicitly named.

### Wednesday — crew plus admissions

- 2:30–5:30: crew.
- 6:30–7:30: SAT through October 2; applications afterward.
- 7:30–9:00: next-due schoolwork.
- 9:00–9:30: pack/reset; no new project rabbit hole.

### Thursday — crew plus production maintenance

- 2:30–5:30: crew.
- 6:30–8:30: next-due schoolwork.
- 8:30–9:15: Melee practice or content capture, chosen before starting.
- 9:15–9:45: content outline/file organization; no full edit.

### Friday — deadline block before work

- 2:15–3:30: SAT through October 2; applications afterward.
- 3:30–4:30: gym/recovery window only if it fits the coach's plan and commute.
- Approximately 5/6–11: paid work. Actual roster replaces this planning window.

### Saturday — fixed load, no invented productivity

- 8:00–11:30: crew.
- Approximately 2/4–11: paid work.
- No scheduled content, applications, gym, or late-night catch-up.

### Sunday — finish one important thing before work

- 9:00–10:30: SAT through October 2; applications afterward.
- 10:30–11:15: weekly review and one named college task.
- Approximately 12/1–9: paid work.
- After work: prepare Monday, then sleep.

## What gets cut first

When a real assignment, race, or extra shift appears, cut in this order:

1. Content administration and optional capture.
2. One Melee block.
3. Gym only when the coach/program says recovery is the constraint.

Do not cut sleep, an application deadline, a teacher's next-due assignment, or
coach-assigned crew work to preserve a YouTube cadence.

## Weekly scoreboard

Sunday review takes no more than 15 minutes:

- School: any missing/late work? Yes/No.
- Applications: one concrete artifact moved forward? Name it.
- Crew: all assigned sessions executed? Record exceptions, not excuses.
- Paid work: actual hours and tip-out logged?
- Content: one deliverable moved a stage (idea → script → record → edit → publish)?
- Melee: number of deliberate sessions, not hours with the game open.
- Sleep: number of school nights at target.

## Revision trigger

Rebuild this schedule when any of these becomes known: confirmed electives,
actual race calendar, spring crew schedule, recurring work roster change,
college application completion, or college move-in/orientation date. The current
calendar intentionally ends November 15 rather than pretending spring is known.
