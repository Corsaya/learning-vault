## Problems 1–2 — Right triangle reference

Right angle at **C**. Side **a** is opposite ∠A, **b** opposite ∠B, **c** is the hypotenuse.

### 1. a = 6, B = 40° → Find b, c, A

$$A = 180° - 90° - 40° = \boxed{50°}$$

$$b = a\tan(B) = 6\tan(40°) \approx \boxed{5.03}$$

$$c = \frac{a}{\cos(B)} = \frac{6}{\cos(40°)} \approx \boxed{7.83}$$

### 2. b = 4, c = 6 → Find a, A, B

$$a = \sqrt{c^2 - b^2} = \sqrt{36 - 16} = \sqrt{20} = 2\sqrt{5} \approx \boxed{4.47}$$

$$B = \arcsin\!\left(\frac{b}{c}\right) = \arcsin\!\left(\frac{4}{6}\right) \approx \boxed{41.8°}$$

$$A = 90° - 41.8° \approx \boxed{48.2°}$$

## Problems 3–6 — Solve

### 3. Hypotenuse = 9 in, one angle = 35° → Find each leg

$$\text{leg opposite } 35° = 9\sin(35°) \approx \boxed{5.16 \text{ in}}$$

$$\text{leg adjacent to } 35° = 9\cos(35°) \approx \boxed{7.37 \text{ in}}$$

### 4. Hypotenuse = 13 in, one leg = 5 in → Find all angles

$$b = \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{144} = 12$$

$$A = \arcsin\!\left(\frac{5}{13}\right) \approx \boxed{22.6°}, \quad B \approx \boxed{67.4°}, \quad C = 90°$$

### 5. Angle = 52°, one leg = 6 in → Find hypotenuse [two answers]

**Case 1** — 6 in is opposite the 52° angle:
$$c = \frac{6}{\sin(52°)} \approx \boxed{7.61 \text{ in}}$$

**Case 2** — 6 in is adjacent to the 52° angle:
$$c = \frac{6}{\cos(52°)} \approx \boxed{9.75 \text{ in}}$$

### 6. Angle = 3π/8 rad, one leg = 4 m → Find hypotenuse [two answers]

$$\frac{3\pi}{8} = 67.5°$$

**Case 1** — 4 m is opposite the 67.5° angle:
$$c = \frac{4}{\sin(67.5°)} \approx \boxed{4.33 \text{ m}}$$

**Case 2** — 4 m is adjacent to the 67.5° angle:
$$c = \frac{4}{\cos(67.5°)} \approx \boxed{10.45 \text{ m}}$$

## Problems 7–14 — Solve

### 7. Tuning fork: d = 15 sin(ωt), frequency = 375 vib/s → Find ω (radians)

For simple harmonic motion, $f = \dfrac{\omega}{2\pi}$, so:

$$\omega = 2\pi f = 2\pi(375) = 750\pi \approx \boxed{2356 \text{ rad/s}}$$

### 8. Find distance A to C across the pond (angle B = 40°, side = 130 yds)

Using Law of Cosines with AB = BC = 130 yds (isoceles setup) and ∠B = 40°:

$$AC^2 = 130^2 + 130^2 - 2(130)(130)\cos(40°)$$
$$= 33800 - 33800(0.766) = 33800 - 25891 = 7909$$
$$AC \approx \boxed{88.9 \text{ yds}}$$

### 9. 80 ft from library. Elevation to flagpole base = 22°, to flag top = 43.5° → Height of flagpole

$$h_{\text{base}} = 80\tan(22°) \approx 32.3 \text{ ft}$$
$$h_{\text{top}} = 80\tan(43.5°) \approx 75.9 \text{ ft}$$

$$\text{Flagpole height} = 75.9 - 32.3 \approx \boxed{44 \text{ ft}}$$

### 10. Lighthouse 320 ft above sea level. Depression angles to two ships: 7° and 10.5° → Distance between ships

$$d_1 = \frac{320}{\tan(10.5°)} \approx 1726 \text{ ft}$$
$$d_2 = \frac{320}{\tan(7°)} \approx 2605 \text{ ft}$$

$$\text{Between ships} = 2605 - 1726 \approx \boxed{879 \text{ ft}}$$

### 11. Wheels: 32 in diameter, 710 rpm → Speed in mph

$$v = 32\pi \;\frac{\text{in}}{\text{rev}} \times 710 \;\frac{\text{rev}}{\text{min}} \times \frac{60\,\text{min}}{1\,\text{hr}} \times \frac{1\,\text{mi}}{63360\,\text{in}}$$

$$= \frac{1{,}363{,}200\pi}{63{,}360} \approx \boxed{67.6 \text{ mph}}$$

> Note: 63,360 in/mi = 5280 ft × 12 in/ft

### 12. Standing 120 ft from bungee platform base, elevation angle = 52° → Platform height

$$h = 120\tan(52°) \approx 120 \times 1.280 \approx \boxed{153.6 \text{ ft}}$$

### 13. Sonar: submarine 6000 ft from ship, depression angle = 36° → Depth

$$\text{depth} = 6000\sin(36°) \approx 6000 \times 0.5878 \approx \boxed{3527 \text{ ft}}$$

### 14. ATV: bearing 52° for 2 hrs, then bearing 142° for 1 hr, all at 20 mph → Distance and bearing from station

**Legs:**
- Leg 1: 40 miles on bearing 52°
- Leg 2: 20 miles on bearing 142°

**Convert to (East, North) components:**

| Leg | East | North |
|-----|------|-------|
| 1 | $40\sin(52°) \approx 31.52$ | $40\cos(52°) \approx 24.63$ |
| 2 | $20\sin(142°) \approx 12.31$ | $20\cos(142°) \approx -15.76$ |
| **Total** | **43.83** | **8.87** |

$$\text{Distance} = \sqrt{43.83^2 + 8.87^2} = \sqrt{2000} \approx \boxed{44.7 \text{ miles}}$$

$$\text{Bearing} = \arctan\!\left(\frac{43.83}{8.87}\right) \approx \arctan(4.94) \approx \boxed{78.5°}$$

The ATV is **44.7 miles** from the station on a bearing of approximately **N 78.5° E**.
