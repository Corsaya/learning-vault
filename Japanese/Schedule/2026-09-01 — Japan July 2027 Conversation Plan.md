# Japan July 2027 Conversation Plan

## Honest target

The target for July 2027 is **functional basic conversation**, not fluency and not an N5 certificate by itself: introduce yourself, order and pay, ask/follow simple directions, explain a simple problem, make small talk with patient speakers, and recover with "please say that again more slowly."

At roughly 7.25 hours/week from September 21 through late June, this supplies about 280 hours plus the bridge work. That is enough for a real beginner foundation if the work includes speaking. It is not enough to promise broad, effortless conversation with native speakers. **Genki I completed, 700-ish core words, and repeated travel-roleplay practice** are the practical outcome to chase.

## September 1–20 bridge: summer work stays first

The active summer-work schedule is already full. After finishing its assigned block each day, do only **20 minutes of Japanese**: kana refresh/katakana, then Anki once it is installed. If the summer-work block ends within 45 minutes of a work shift, this 20 minutes is the whole discretionary block; do not create a late-night debt.

Melee is not assigned a daily block during this bridge. Use the existing OEM-controller first-week plan only on days with a genuine 45-minute window after summer work. The bootcamp starts September 21, after the reading deadline.

### Bridge checklist

- Sep 1–7: refresh hiragana; learn katakana rows ア–ノ.
- Sep 8–14: finish katakana rows ハ–ン; drill dakuten, small characters, and long-vowel mark.
- Sep 15–20: 20-minute mixed kana speed drills; install Anki; make the first 20 travel/core-vocabulary cards.

## Speaking requirement

From Genki I chapter 3 onward, the Sunday block must include **actual speech**, not silent written answers: read a dialogue aloud, shadow 5–10 lines, then answer its questions without reading. From January, replace half of these blocks with a 30-minute conversation partner/tutor or a voice conversation with a patient Japanese speaker.

## Naruto rule

Start only once katakana is automatic (estimated October 19). Read **two panels or one page, maximum 15 minutes, every other Saturday**. First read without a dictionary; then look up only words that recur or make the sentence intelligible. Add at most **two cards**. Naruto's casual speech, fighting vocabulary, and omitted subjects are exposure, not a template for how to speak to people in Japan.

## Travel-can-do scoreboard

By June, be able to do each without a script:

- greet, introduce yourself, and say where you are from;
- order a meal, state a preference/allergy only if actually needed, and pay;
- ask whether a train or bus goes to a place and understand a short answer;
- ask for an item, size, price, or availability in a shop;
- say you do not understand, ask for repetition, and use a translation app politely;
- make two minutes of simple small talk about school, travel, hobbies, or food.
