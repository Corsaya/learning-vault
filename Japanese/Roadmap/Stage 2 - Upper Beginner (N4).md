# Stage 2 — Upper Beginner (N4)

**Goal:** Finish "beginner" Japanese. Complete the core grammar that the rest of the
language builds on, double your vocab and kanji, and shift the balance toward reading
real (graded) material. **This is the last fully textbook-driven stage.**

**Hours:** to ~800 cumulative · **ETA:** ~2.2 yr @7hr · ~1.5 yr @10hr

> [!note] Your free-resource line
> You said: free until N4 proficiency. N4 is where you finish the **free** Tae Kim /
> Genki path. After this, paid tools (Bunpro, WaniKani, italki) become *optional
> accelerators*, never requirements. See [[Paid Options Worth It]].

## What you'll be able to do (can-do)

- Understand everyday conversation at near-natural speed on familiar topics
- Read short essays, simple manga (with furigana), all of NHK Easy with few lookups
- Use te-form fully, plain vs. polite, conditionals, giving/receiving, casual speech
- Follow slice-of-life anime with Japanese subtitles + occasional lookups

## Targets

| Thing | Target |
|---|---|
| Grammar | All N4 grammar (Genki II ch. 13–23 OR Tae Kim "Essential Grammar") |
| Vocab | ~1,500 total |
| Kanji (recognition) | ~300 total |
| Reading | Tadoku L2–L3, easy manga w/ furigana, NHK Easy fluently |
| Listening | Intermediate CI, slice-of-life anime w/ JP subs |

## The spine

- Finish **Genki II** (chapters 13–23) *or* **Tae Kim essential grammar**.
- Keep **vocab SRS** rolling; graduate from the starter deck to an **N4 vocab deck** (free Anki decks exist) or start **mining** from what you read.
- **Bunpro** through N4 grammar if you want SRS'd grammar (free trial / paid; the free analog is reviewing your own grammar notes in Anki).

## Weekly emphasis

~35% SRS · 20% grammar · 40% input · 5% output. **Input overtakes grammar here** —
this is the intended shift. Read more, drill grammar less.

## Start sentence mining (the skill that carries you to N1)

Once N4 grammar is mostly done, begin **sentence mining**:
1. Read/watch native-ish content with **Yomitan** (browser) or a manga reader.
2. When you hit a sentence you *almost* understand except one word, save it.
3. SRS the sentence (comprehension card — read it, recall the meaning).

This is the engine of the Refold/immersion method and how most self-taught fluent
learners actually got there. See [[Free Resource Stack]] → Sentence Mining.

## Handwriting / speaking check-in

- **Handwriting:** if you're keeping the skill track, you're now writing ~300 kanji range. Keep it to a small weekly slice; recognition still leads.
- **Speaking (your #5):** still optional. If you ever want it, this is the earliest sane point to add light shadowing (repeat aloud after CI videos). No pressure.

## Milestone checklist

- [ ] Finished N4 grammar (Genki II or Tae Kim essential)
- [ ] ~1,500 vocab in SRS
- [ ] ~300 kanji recognized
- [ ] Started a sentence-mining deck (even 1–2 cards/day)
- [ ] Read 10+ Tadoku L2–L3 readers and a full easy manga volume
- [ ] Watch a slice-of-life anime episode with JP subtitles and follow the gist
- [ ] A free N4 mock test feels comfortable

## Done when…

N4 grammar complete, ~1,500 words, ~300 kanji, reading easy native material and
mining from it, free N4 mock comfortable. → [[Roadmap/Stage 3 - Intermediate (N3)|Stage 3]].
