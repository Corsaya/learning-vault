# Stage 3 — Intermediate (N3)

**Goal:** Cross the bridge from "studying Japanese" to "using Japanese." N3 is the
real-world threshold — the level where native content starts to open up and where
**immersion becomes your primary teacher**. It's also where most people quit. Don't.

**Hours:** to ~1,400 cumulative · **ETA:** ~3.8 yr @7hr · ~2.7 yr @10hr

> [!warning] The plateau lives here
> N3→N2 *feels* slow because new words appear less often in any single source. This
> is normal and expected — see [[Hours & Timeline Reality Check]]. Your daily-SRS
> habit, built in earlier stages, is what carries you through. Trust the process.

## What you'll be able to do (can-do)

- Understand everyday conversation and much of slice-of-life anime without subtitles
- Read manga comfortably, light novels with effort, full NHK Easy effortlessly
- Start on **regular NHK News** (your "formal reading" priority begins here)
- Hold a basic conversation if you choose to practice output

## Targets

| Thing | Target |
|---|---|
| Grammar | All N3 grammar |
| Vocab | ~3,000–3,750 total |
| Kanji (recognition) | ~650 total |
| Reading | Manga fluently, light novels w/ lookups, NHK regular (slowly) |
| Listening | Slice-of-life anime w/o subs, intermediate podcasts |

## The spine — shift to immersion-led

The textbook era is ending. Structure now comes from **graded grammar + heavy mining**:
- **Grammar:** an N3 grammar resource (Bunpro N3, **Tobira: Gateway to Advanced** is the college-standard third-year book and starts around here, or a free N3 grammar list reviewed via SRS).
- **Vocab:** primarily **mined** from your immersion now, supplemented by an N3 frequency deck.
- **Immersion:** this is the main event. Pick a show/manga/game *you love* and live in it.

## Weekly emphasis

~30% SRS · 15% grammar · 50% input · 5% output. **Half your time is now reading +
listening to real content.** This is the correct, research-backed shift.

## "Formal reading" begins (your #4 priority)

Start easing into **regular NHK News Web** (not Easy). It'll be hard. Read one article
slowly per week with Yomitan, mine 2–3 words. By end of N3 it should feel *doable*.

## Output decision point (your #5 priority)

You ranked speaking last and may take JLPT only if pursuing it 18+. Totally fine to
stay input-only. **But** if you ever want speaking, N3 is the sweet spot to start —
you finally have enough to say things. Free options: **HelloTalk** (language exchange),
**Tandem**, or shadowing. Paid: **italki** tutors (see [[Paid Options Worth It]]).
Optional. Skippable. Your call.

## Milestone checklist

- [ ] Finished N3 grammar
- [ ] ~3,000+ vocab (much of it mined from real content)
- [ ] ~650 kanji recognized
- [ ] Mining 3–5 cards/day from native material consistently
- [ ] Finished a full manga series OR ~10 anime episodes mostly without subs
- [ ] Read 4+ regular (non-Easy) NHK articles
- [ ] A free N3 mock test feels comfortable
- [ ] (If 18+ and committed) consider registering for the real N3 — first external checkpoint

## Done when…

N3 grammar done, ~3,000 words, living in native content daily, NHK regular feels
approachable. → [[Roadmap/Stage 4 - Upper Intermediate (N2)|Stage 4]].
