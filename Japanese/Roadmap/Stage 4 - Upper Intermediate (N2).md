# Stage 4 — Upper Intermediate (N2)

**Goal:** Reach the level the real world actually asks for. **N2 is the practical
fluency bar** — most jobs, universities, and "I speak Japanese" claims mean N2. If you
stop anywhere short of N1, stop here proud. Native content becomes genuinely enjoyable.

**Hours:** to ~2,200 cumulative · **ETA:** ~6.0 yr @7hr · ~4.2 yr @10hr

## What you'll be able to do (can-do)

- Understand most native media (anime, dramas, YouTube, podcasts) without subtitles
- Read novels, news, and most manga with occasional lookups
- Follow nuanced/abstract conversation
- Read NHK regular news comfortably — your "formal reading" goal, achieved

## Targets

| Thing | Target |
|---|---|
| Grammar | All N2 grammar (Tobira / Shin Kanzen Master N2 / Quartet) |
| Vocab | ~6,000 total |
| Kanji (recognition) | ~1,000 total |
| Reading | Novels w/ lookups, news fluently, light novels comfortably |
| Listening | Native media without subs as default |

## The spine — mostly immersion + targeted gap-filling

- **Grammar:** **Shin Kanzen Master N2** or **Quartet** for the trickier N2 grammar/reading. These are *targeted reference*, not a daily textbook.
- **Vocab:** overwhelmingly **mined**. A frequency deck for gaps. By now you add words from novels, news, and the shows you watch.
- **Immersion:** the bulk of your time. Read widely; listen daily.

## Weekly emphasis

~25% SRS · 10% grammar · 60% input · 5% output. **You're mostly living in Japanese now,**
mining what you don't know, occasionally consulting grammar references.

## Reading ladder (your #1 priority pays off)

Push the reading harder here — it's your top skill and N2's reading section is dense:
1. Light novels / easy novels (e.g., Yotsuba& → easy LNs → general fiction)
2. Regular NHK + a topic you care about (sports, tech, gaming) in Japanese
3. Start a **first "real" novel** — slow, with lookups. Finishing one is a milestone.

## Milestone checklist

- [ ] Finished N2 grammar
- [ ] ~6,000 vocab
- [ ] ~1,000 kanji recognized
- [ ] Default-watching native media without subtitles
- [ ] Read a full novel (with lookups)
- [ ] Read NHK regular news comfortably
- [ ] A free N2 mock feels comfortable
- [ ] (If pursuing it) consider taking the real N2 — the credential most worth having

## A fork in the road

> [!tip] N2 is a legitimate finish line
> If at N2 you're happily consuming everything you want and don't need the credential,
> **you can stop here and call it a win** — ~6 years @7hr, ~4 @10hr. N1 is another
> ~1,400 hours for diminishing real-world return (mostly academic/literary nuance and
> the prestige of the credential). Decide based on whether N1 still excites you.

## Done when…

N2 grammar done, ~6,000 words, native media is your default, news is comfortable,
free N2 mock comfortable. → [[Roadmap/Stage 5 - Advanced (N1)|Stage 5]] (if you choose to).
