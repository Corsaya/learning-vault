# Japanese 日本語

Learning Japanese from beginner to JLPT N1. A multi-year project, paced at
**7 hours/week** (the sustainable default) with a **10 hours/week committed track**
that reaches N1 in ~7–8 years. Reading-first, media-driven, self-study with Claude
as curriculum and NotebookLM for audio/video output.

## The one-line summary

Build a daily SRS habit (vocab + kanji recognition), add grammar in small bites,
and spend the rest of your time *reading and listening to real Japanese* slightly
above your level. Everything below is the detailed version of that sentence.

  so the roadmap stays accurate at any pace.
