# Free Resource Stack

Everything you need to reach N1 can be free. This is the curated stack — not a giant
list, just the ones worth using, in the order you'll use them. All **$0** unless noted.

> [!tip] Don't collect resources — use these
> The #1 beginner trap is hoarding apps. Pick one per category and go. This stack is
> already the pick.

## 1. Kana (Stage 0)

- **[Tofugu Hiragana](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese/learn-hiragana/) + [Katakana](https://www.tofugu.com/japanese/learn-katakana/) guides** — mnemonic-based, the standard free way in.
- **[RealKana](https://realkana.com/) / [Kana Pro](https://kana.pro/)** — drill to instant recognition.

## 2. SRS — the backbone (all stages)

- **[Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/)** — free on desktop, web, and Android. **iOS app is $25** (one-time) — see [[Paid Options Worth It]] for the workaround. This is the single most important tool in the whole plan.
- **Starter deck:** **Kaishi 1.5k** (free, the modern recommended beginner vocab deck — replaces the old "Core" decks).
- **[Yomitan](https://yomitan.wiki/)** — browser extension, instant hover-dictionary, makes Anki cards in one click. Essential for reading + mining.

## 3. Grammar (Stages 1–4)

- **[Tae Kim's Guide to Japanese](https://guidetojapanese.org/learn/)** — completely free, full beginner→intermediate grammar. The free spine.
- **Genki I & II** — the college standard (most US universities use it for year 1–2). Not free to buy, but *the* structured path if you want a textbook. Libraries/PDFs exist.
- **[Bunpro](https://bunpro.jp/)** — SRS'd grammar, N5→N1 paths. Free trial then paid; the free substitute is putting grammar points into Anki yourself.
- **[Imabi](https://imabi.org/)** — free, extremely deep grammar reference for later levels.

## 4. Kanji (recognition-first)

- **Learn kanji *inside vocab*** via your SRS — the recommended default. No separate kanji grind.
- **[WaniKani](https://www.wanikani.com/)** — free to level 3, then paid. Popular dedicated kanji SRS (same team as Tofugu). Optional; see [[Paid Options Worth It]].
- **[jpdb.io](https://jpdb.io/)** — free, frequency-based vocab/kanji decks you can build from media.

## 5. Reading (your #1 priority)

- **[Tadoku free graded readers](https://tadoku.org/japanese/en/free-books-en/)** — leveled, free PDFs from absolute beginner up. Start here.
- **[NHK News Web Easy](https://www3.nhk.or.jp/news/easy/)** — real news rewritten simply with furigana. The N4→N3 bridge.
- **[Satori Reader](https://www.satorireader.com/)** — graded reading w/ built-in dictionary + audio (free tier, paid full). Excellent N3-ish.
- **Manga + Yomitan / [ttsu reader](https://reader.ttsu.app/)** — read native manga/ebooks with instant lookups.
- **Regular NHK / Asahi / Yomiuri** — "formal reading," N3 onward.

## 6. Listening (your #2 priority)

- **[Comprehensible Japanese](https://www.youtube.com/@cijapanese)** (YouTube) — graded comprehensible input, beginner→advanced, free.
- **Nihongo con Teppei** (podcast) — graded, free, hundreds of episodes.
- **[YouGlish Japanese](https://youglish.com/japanese)** — hear any word in real native clips.
- **Anime/dramas with Japanese subtitles** — from N3 on, your main listening engine.
- **NotebookLM** — turn your grammar notes into audio reviews for passive listening (see [[Weekly Routine]]).

## 7. Sentence mining (N4 onward — the carry-to-N1 skill)

- Read/watch with **Yomitan** → save sentences with one unknown word → SRS them.
- **[Refold Japanese roadmap](https://refold.la/roadmap)** — free guide to the immersion/mining method many self-taught fluent learners used.
- **[Migaku](https://migaku.com/)** — browser/Netflix mining tool (paid) — optional accelerator.

## 8. Dictionaries & reference (always)

- **[Jisho.org](https://jisho.org/)** — the standard free dictionary.
- **[Yomitan]** (above) — for in-line lookups.
- **[jpdb.io]** — frequency data + reading tools.

## 9. Testing / self-assessment (free)

- **[Official JLPT sample questions](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/samples/forlearners.html)** — free, all levels.
- **[JLPT Sensei](https://jlptsensei.com/)** — free grammar/vocab lists + practice by level.

---

## The minimum viable stack (if you want just ONE of each)

1. **Anki** + Kaishi 1.5k deck (SRS)
2. **Tae Kim** (grammar)
3. **Yomitan** (lookups + mining)
4. **Tadoku → NHK Easy** (reading)
5. **Comprehensible Japanese** (listening)

That's a complete, free, N1-capable toolkit. Everything else is optional polish.
