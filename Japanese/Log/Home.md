# Japanese — Study Log

Daily/weekly study entries. ISO dates: `YYYY-MM-DD.md` (same convention as the Crew
training log). Use the **[[Japanese Study Log]]** template (`_Templates/`).

The point isn't detail — it's the **visible streak**. One line counts. The log is
what carries you across the years.

## How to use

- New entry per study day (or per week if you prefer): `YYYY-MM-DD.md`
- Log at least: minutes, what you did, SRS done (y/n), one thing learned
- Sunday: glance at [[Master Checklist]], note next focus

## Quick template

```
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [japanese, log]
stage: Stage 1 — N5
minutes: 60
---

# Japanese — Mon, Month DD, YYYY

- **SRS:** ✅ (N reviews)
- **Did:** grammar ch.X · read NHK Easy · 1 CI video
- **Mined:** 3 cards
- **Learned:** one word/grammar/insight
- **Next:** ...
```
