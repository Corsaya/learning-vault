## The vision, in one paragraph

A personal assistant that searches repos and social media to improve itself,
holds its own against the other Jarvis-esque projects, talks back with an AI
voice, and installs on the laptop as well as the PC. It eases access to the
computer, creates and organizes the codebase, and builds lessons and plans —
physical, educational, and goal-level — while assistant and owner improve
each other. Endgame: learn to build a model outside Claude, and earn enough
to fund the whole thing.
