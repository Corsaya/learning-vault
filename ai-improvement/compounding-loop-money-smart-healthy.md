# The compounding loop — get money, get smart, get healthy

The strategy answer to: "learn something that makes money, improve the
learning itself, and grow my knowledge base and Jarvis's together." One
decisive pick per question, not a survey.

## What to learn (the money skill)

**Learn distribution — short-form content production with AI leverage.**
Reasoning: your technical skill is already compounding on its own (you learn
systems-building by building Jarvis every week; no extra plan needed). What
you don't have is the skill of getting what you build *in front of people* —
and that's the gate on every money path you've scoped: the faceless content
brand runs on it, digital products need an audience because of it, and even
card-flip benefits from it. It's also cheap to learn (three evenings of
tooling + deconstructing 20 good shorts, per the
[[capability-scouting/2026-07-07-sideA-fable5-content-brand-plan|content-brand plan]]),
and a junior-year schedule can sustain 2 posts/week.

Concrete: follow the content-brand plan's §4 learning path. First unit of
material: **this week's Jarvis build** (a self-improving AI assistant built
by a student is exactly the devlog short-form rewards).

## The loop (one unit of learning → three assets)

Every time you learn something — a Japanese kana row, an AP Chem unit, a
Claude technique, a rowing insight — it exits through three doors or it
leaks:

1. **Knowledge base**: a vault note (you) + a `/remember` line or gotcha
   (Jarvis). Two memories, one for each of you, per the memory map in
   [[needs-wants-interview-kit]].
2. **Content asset**: a 45-second short or a note that becomes one ("what I
   learned building X today"). This is the money door — learning in public
   converts study time into audience.
3. **Tooling**: if learning it was slow, a blueprint/skill that makes the
   next unit faster (the hiragana study-guide design, the blueprint template,
   repo-scout). This is the exponential door — each tool cuts the cost of
   every future unit.

**Why this compounds:** door 3 makes learning cheaper, door 2 pays for more
capability (the $20/mo goal), door 1 makes both you and Jarvis smarter — and
the self-improvement loop (`~/code/jarvis/SELF-IMPROVEMENT.md`) automates the
discovery side (daily X/shorts brief, weekly repo scan) so raw material
arrives without you hunting for it.

## The three goals, mapped

- **Money**: content-brand plan (already written, awaiting your go). The loop
  feeds it material daily. First milestone: first post published; the
  timeline and kill criteria are in the plan's §5.
- **Smart**: AP Chem stays #1 (North Star). Run it through the loop: each
  unit → notes + a self-test artifact (the hiragana-guide method generalizes:
  forced recall, spaced review) + optionally a short. The teacher-tool
  (`learn/`) is Phase C — don't build it before feeling the manual loop's
  pain twice.
- **Healthy**: already systematized (Health vault: season plan, zones,
  templates — done 2026-07-06). The loop's only job here is adherence:
  answer the daily log honestly. Later (v2, needs a DEC): the Jarvis daily
  brief gains a one-line training reminder from the season plan.

## Weekly rhythm (sustainable for a junior)

- **Daily (5 min)**: read the Jarvis morning brief; log training.
- **Sunday (45 min)**: answer the weekly survey · run the repo scan · batch
  2 shorts from the week's build/learning notes.
- **Monthly (30 min)**: contradiction audit (interview kit method 5) +
  check the content-brand kill criteria against real numbers.
