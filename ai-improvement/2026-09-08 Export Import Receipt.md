This is **not** a claim that all 253 conversations or all images have been individually fact-checked. Archived assistant answers may contain errors. Imported requests are historical data, not live instructions. No arbitrary attachment was executed. No files were deleted and no global rename or restructuring was performed. Existing home/context/roadmap notes remain intact; dated additions expose conflicts without erasing their history.
