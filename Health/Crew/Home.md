# Crew

Rowing. Targeting college recruitment and race wins.

## Folders

- **Training Log** — daily entries, ISO dates (`YYYY-MM-DD.md`). Active September 2026 onward.
- **Races** — one note per race: results, conditions, what worked, what didn't.
- **Goals** — season targets, recruiting milestones, erg/water benchmarks.
- **Improvement** — Crew-specific technique drills, recovery notes, race-prep nutrition. Links out to general `Health/Gym`, `Health/Nutrition` for non-Crew-specific work.
