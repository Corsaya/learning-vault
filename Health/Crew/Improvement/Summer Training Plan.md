# Summer Training Plan 2026

Master document. Links out to [[Erg Workouts]], [[On-Water Technique]], [[Strength Program]], [[Bulk Protocol]].

## Objective
Build the aerobic engine and physical size that makes the sub-5:53 possible. Summer is not race prep — it is the foundation everything else runs on. No anaerobic work. No shortcuts.

## Constraints
- **Lifeguarding starts June 7** — limits morning availability. All sessions shift to afternoon/evening.
- On-water access depends on club schedule — erg is the primary volume tool when 1x isn't available.
- Training journal goes live September 2026. Use this file and the daily log for notes until then.

---

## Phase Overview

### Phase 0 — Pre-Lifeguard (now → June 6)
Doubles possible. Morning erg + afternoon gym or reverse. Use this window to lock in lift technique before volume ramps.
- Erg: 5–6 sessions/week, 60–75 min UT2
- Gym: 3x/week, technique priority
- On-water: as available

### Phase 1 — Base Build (June 7 → July 20)
Single sessions. Aerobic volume accumulation. Lift heavy 3x/week.
- Erg: 4–5 sessions/week, 60–90 min UT2 @ r18–20
- Gym: 3x/week (Mon/Thu + one weekend day)
- On-water: 1–2x/week when available, technique only
- No pieces harder than UT1

### Phase 2 — Base Consolidation (July 21 → Aug 31)
Same structure. Introduce occasional UT1 work (max 20% of volume). Watch bodyweight and recovery.
- Erg: 4–5 sessions/week — 80% UT2, 20% UT1
- Gym: 3x/week, begin adding load systematically
- On-water: 1–2x/week
- Begin fall build planning last week of August

---

## Weekly Skeleton (Phase 1 & 2)

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Monday | Gym — heavy (deadlift focus) |
| Tuesday | Erg — long UT2 (75–90 min) |
| Wednesday | On-water 1x — technique, or erg 60 min if no water |
| Thursday | Gym — moderate (squat/pull focus) |
| Friday | Erg — UT2 (60 min) |
| Saturday | On-water 1x or long erg (75+ min) |
| Sunday | Rest / active recovery (walk, stretch, foam roll) |

Adjust gym days if lifting leaves legs too dead for the next day's erg.

---

## Guiding Principles
1. **Rate cap r20 all summer.** Power per stroke, not strokes per minute.
2. **80/20 rule.** 80% easy (UT2), 20% moderate (UT1). Zero hard until fall.
3. **Food is training.** Missing calories = missing adaptation. Hit the surplus every day.
4. **Sleep 8–9 hours.** The weight doesn't go on and the engine doesn't build without it.
5. **Log what you eat and lift.** You can't manage what you don't track.

- Gym shifts from hypertrophy to strength/power
- Racing season structure resumes
