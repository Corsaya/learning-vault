# Erg Workouts

Summer focus: **aerobic base**. Long. Slow. Low rate. Volume is the product.
All work is UT2 or UT1 — no anaerobic pieces until fall.

## Rate Targets
| Zone | Rate | Effort | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| UT2 | r18–20 | 65–75% | Could hold a conversation. Nose breathing possible. |
| UT1 | r20–22 | 75–82% | Controlled. Breathing elevated but steady. |

Use a metronome or Concept2 rate cage if rate creep is an issue.

---

## UT2 Sessions (80% of summer volume)

### SS-60
60 min continuous @ r18
Straightforward. Focus on ratio and blade prep, not split.

### SS-75
75 min continuous @ r18–20
The standard summer session. Settle into a rhythm by minute 15.

### SS-90
90 min continuous @ r18–20
Long effort. Eat something beforehand. Hydrate.

### 3×20 (rest 3 min paddle)
3 × 20 min @ r18 / 3 min easy paddle between
Good for days when sitting continuous feels too flat. Total ~66 min of work.

### 4×15 (rest 2 min paddle)
4 × 15 min @ r18 / 2 min rest
Slightly more structured. Total 60 min of work.

---

## UT1 Sessions (20% of summer volume — Phase 2 only)

### 2×20 @ UT1
2 × 20 min @ r20 / 5 min rest
First time introducing UT1 — start here. Should feel controlled, not labored.

### 3×15 @ UT1
3 × 15 min @ r20 / 4 min rest
Standard UT1 session. If breathing is ragged, back off rate or intensity.

### 45 min continuous @ UT1
Continuous version for variety. Harder to pace — use HR if possible.

---

## Monitoring
- **Split:** Note average split per session but don't chase it. Summer splits will be slow. That's correct.
- **Heart rate:** UT2 = ~145–160 bpm (estimate — calibrate to feel if no monitor).
- **Drag factor:** Set 110–130 for sculling specificity.

## Notes
- If anything feels like a piece, you're going too hard.
- Tired from lifting? Drop to SS-60 or take the day off. Don't dig a hole.
- On-water days count as UT2 work — don't stack a full erg on top.
