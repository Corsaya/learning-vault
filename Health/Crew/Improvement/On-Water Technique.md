# On-Water Technique — 1x Focus

Summer on-water sessions are technique only. No hard pieces. Rate cap r18–20.
Each session picks **one or two focus areas** — don't try to fix everything at once.

---

## Core Principles

**Drive sequencing:** Legs → body swing → arms. In that order, every stroke.
**Recovery sequencing:** Arms away → body over → legs up. Unhurried.
**Ratio:** The recovery should take at least 2× as long as the drive. Feel the pause at the finish.
**Blade work:** Tap down cleanly. Square early. No splash at the catch. Feather fully before the hands move away.

---

## Drill Library

### Pause Drills
Pause at specific points to feel position and balance.
- **Catch pause** — pause just before entry, check blade height and posture
- **Finish pause** — pause at the draw, feel the squeeze, check handle height
- **Arms-away pause** — pause when arms are extended, force body to lead separately
- **Bodies-over pause** — pause when body is rocked over, before legs break

### Ratio & Recovery
- **Slow-recovery 20** — r14 on the stroke, full slow recovery. Exaggerate the pause.
- **Pick drill** — arms only → arms + body → full stroke. Build from the finish out.
- **One-foot balance** — row with one foot out of the stretcher (briefly) to expose reliance on grip for balance

### Blade Precision
- **Tap-and-feather drill** — exaggerate the tap-down and feather at the finish, slow water
- **Square blade rowing** — keep blades squared through entire recovery, forces clean entry
- **Half-slide entry focus** — row at half-slide only, focus entirely on blade entry

### Drive Mechanics
- **Legs-only** — arms and body fixed, drive only with legs. Feel leg connection.
- **Legs + body** — add the body swing once legs are extended, no arm pull yet
- **Suspension drill** — think "hang" off the stretcher at the catch, connect before pulling

---

## Session Structure (suggested)
1. 10 min easy paddle to warm up and settle the boat
2. 2–3 drill sets on the focus area (3 min drill / 3 min normal rowing)
3. 20–40 min steady-state at r18, applying the focus area
4. Cool-down paddle, note what held and what broke down

---

## Focus Rotation (summer)
Cycle through these week to week rather than staying on one indefinitely.

| Week focus | Priority |
|---|---|
| Ratio & recovery | Feel the pause, slow the slide |
| Catch timing | Square early, no splash |
| Drive sequencing | Legs lead, body follows |
| Balance & set | Don't grip for balance |
| Finish | Clean extraction, full draw |

---

## Notes
- Film yourself from the dock when possible. Hard to feel what you can't see.
- If the boat is moving badly, go back to basics: pause drills, slow rate.
- Technique at r18 is the same technique you need at r32. Build it slow.
