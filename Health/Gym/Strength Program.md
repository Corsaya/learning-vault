# Strength Program — Summer 2026

3 days per week. Concurrent with erg/water volume.
Summer phase = **hypertrophy focus** (volume, moderate load, full ROM).
Fall phase shifts to strength/power — update this file when the time comes.

Schedule fits into: **Monday (heavy), Thursday (moderate), Saturday (optional/lighter)**.
Adjust days if erg sessions are suffering — recovery is the constraint.

---

## Day A — Monday (Deadlift Focus)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Deadlift | 4 × 5 | Work up to a top set. Brace hard, hinge from hips. |
| Romanian Deadlift (RDL) | 3 × 8 | Moderate load, full hip extension at top, control the descent. |
| Weighted Pull-ups | 4 × 5–8 | Add weight via belt. Full hang at bottom, chin over bar. |
| Pallof Press | 3 × 12 each side | Anti-rotation core. Slow and controlled. |
| Copenhagen Plank | 3 × 20 sec each side | Adductor + lateral core. |

---

## Day B — Thursday (Squat Focus)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Front Squat | 4 × 5 | Upright torso, elbows high. Quad + core demand. |
| Romanian Deadlift (RDL) | 3 × 8 | Same as Day A — posterior chain maintenance. |
| Weighted Pull-ups | 3 × 5–6 | Slightly lower volume than Monday. |
| Dead Bug | 3 × 10 each side | Lower back stays pinned to floor throughout. |
| Bird Dog | 3 × 10 each side | Slow and controlled. Opposite arm/leg. |

---

## Day C — Saturday (Optional / Lighter)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Front Squat | 3 × 5 | Lighter than Thursday. Technique and feel. |
| Pull-ups (unweighted) | 3 × max | Accumulate volume. |
| RDL | 2 × 10 | Light, feel-based. |
| Core circuit (choice) | 2 rounds | Pick 2–3 from: plank, hollow hold, Pallof, Copenhagen |

Skip Day C if legs are dead from the week. Rest > junk volume.

---

## Progression Scheme
- **Deadlift / Front Squat:** Add 5 lbs when all sets are completed with good form. Don't chase numbers at the expense of technique.
- **RDL:** Add weight when 8 reps feel easy across all sets.
- **Weighted pull-ups:** Add 2.5–5 lbs when top set hits 8 clean reps.
- **Core work:** Progress to harder variations over time, not just more reps.

## Key Cues
- **Deadlift:** Lat engagement before you pull ("protect your armpits"). Bar stays in contact with legs.
- **RDL:** Push hips back, not down. Feel the hamstring load, not the lower back.
- **Front Squat:** Elbows up is non-negotiable. Lose the rack position and the lift falls apart.
- **Pull-ups:** Dead hang at the bottom. No kipping. Scapular retraction starts the pull.
- **Core anti-rotation:** Move slow. If you're shaking, you're working. If you're compensating, you're not.

---

## Fall Shift
When fall arrives, transition to lower rep ranges (3–5), higher loads, and introduce power work (power clean, jump work if accessible). Update this file at that point.
