# Bulk Protocol — Summer 2026

Target: **160 → 180 lbs** of functional muscle by spring 2027.
That's ~20 lbs over ~10 months — ~2 lbs/month. Achievable with a clean bulk. Not a dirty bulk.

---

## Calorie Targets

| | Estimate |
|---|---|
| TDEE (current, high activity) | ~3,000–3,200 cal |
| Daily surplus | +300–500 cal |
| **Target intake** | **3,300–3,700 cal/day** |

Start at 3,400. Weigh yourself weekly (same time, same conditions — morning, after bathroom).
- Gaining less than 1 lb/2 weeks → add 150–200 cal
- Gaining more than 1 lb/week consistently → pull back 100–150 cal

---

## Macro Targets

| Macro | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | **200–220g/day** | ~1.25–1.4g/lb bodyweight. Priority #1. |
| Carbohydrates | **400–500g/day** | Fuel for training. Don't cut carbs. |
| Fat | **80–100g/day** | Hormone support, joint health. |

Protein is the hard one to hit consistently. Plan around it — protein first, then fill carbs and fat around it.

---

## Meal Timing

**Pre-training (60–90 min before):**
Carb-focused, moderate protein, low fat, low fiber. Easy to digest.
Examples: oats + protein powder, rice + chicken, bagel + peanut butter.

**Post-training (within 45 min):**
Protein + carbs. Recovery window. Don't skip this.
Examples: protein shake + banana, chicken + rice, Greek yogurt + granola.

**Before bed:**
Slow-digesting protein. Supports overnight muscle protein synthesis.
Examples: cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, casein shake, whole milk.

---

## Food List

**Protein sources:**
Eggs, chicken breast/thighs, ground beef (90/10), canned tuna/salmon, Greek yogurt (plain), cottage cheese, whole milk, protein powder (whey or whey-casein blend)

**Carb sources:**
White rice, oats, sweet potato, whole grain bread, pasta, fruit (bananas, apples, berries), granola, cereal

**Fat sources:**
Avocado, olive oil, peanut butter / almond butter, whole eggs, whole milk, nuts

**Vegetables (volume, micronutrients):**
Spinach, broccoli, mixed greens, peppers, cucumber — eat them, but don't let them crowd out calorie-dense foods

## Supplements (Optional)
- **Creatine monohydrate** — 5g/day, any time. Most evidence-backed supplement for power and muscle gain. Load or skip loading (just start at 5g/day).
- **Protein powder** — only if hitting 200g/day from whole food is consistently difficult.
- **Nothing else is necessary.** Don't waste money on pre-workout stacks.

## Notes
- **Lifeguarding 9am–6pm, 5 days/week (June 7 – Sep 7)** — see [[Lifeguarding Schedule]]. Sun + standing = elevated sweat loss; add electrolytes on work days. Meal timing shifts: pre-training meal after work (6pm+) on those days.
- Hunger is a good sign early in the bulk. Your appetite will adjust up over weeks.
- If weight stalls for 2+ weeks and you're eating the target, add 200 cal — don't guess, track.
- Sleep is when growth hormone peaks. 8–9 hours is part of the protocol.
