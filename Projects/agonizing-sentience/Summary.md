# agonizing-sentience — Summary

A collaborative novel written with a friend. Two-voice project: Donovan and his
friend are the only authors. The shared, canonical work lives in the public repo
(`~/Documents/Obsidian/agonizing-sentience/`); this folder is Donovan's private
scratchpad for ideas before they're pitched.

## Status (as of 2026-05-30)

- **Chapter 3 in progress** (per vault context).
- Two versions of the opening exist in the public repo: a "ReRun" (V1) and
  "Mist Shroud" (V2), with matching storyboards.
- Brainstorming and plans are expanding.

## How this scratchpad fits in

1. Idea lands here, half-baked.
2. You shape it — privately, in your own voice.
3. If it's worth sharing, you pitch it and it moves to the public repo.

Nothing here is binding on the shared story until it's been pitched and agreed on.
